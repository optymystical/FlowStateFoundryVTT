# **Perception**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules and action:**

- **Senses:** Counter to stealth, perception is one's ability to sense the world around them in various ways, and they use perception through one of the three main senses below (more can be found in Mental/Magic). Each one of your senses can be Secondary, Primary, or Heightened. Some effects make a sense count as one level higher or lower (Secondary ↔ Primary ↔ Heightened), and a sense lowered below Secondary does not work until that effect ends. If you have no Primary or Heightened senses, all actions that rely on senses are made with Disadvantage, and if all of your senses are none of the three, then you cannot make any actions that require senses. Each sense’s table shows when it needs a check to perceive something, and when it passes automatically.  
- **Sight (Default Primary):** Your ability to physically see targets. Dim light lightly obscures what you see, while darkness or harsh, blinding light heavily obscures it. After 1 hour of uninterrupted time in the same lighting, your eyes adjust, treating it as one step less obscured (heavily → lightly → none) until you leave it. While Blinded, your Sight does not work.

- **Hearing (Default Primary):** Your ability to physically hear targets. When the sound you are trying to single out is covered by other sounds of similar or greater loudness, your Hearing counts as one level lower for that check. While Deafened, your Hearing does not work.

- **Smell (Default Secondary):** Your ability to physically smell targets. While among an overpowering odor (such as rot, smoke, or sewage), your Smell counts as one level lower against any other smell.

- **Spot:** At any time a target is within your senses, you can spend 1 AP to use the Spot action, which lets you make a perception check against their stealth check.  
    
  **Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Glimpse:** You can spend **2 RP** to attempt to spot a target that is hidden from you, so long as they have done something this round to alert you. Once used on the target, this cannot be used again on them until they alert you. Follows standard perceive vs stealth check rules.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Vigilance:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** When combat begins and you would normally be unaware, you may use this to immediately become aware, and you are not surprised.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Focus:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When the result of a perception check you make would be less than half of your die’s maximum, you can use this ability to reroll the check. You must take the new result, and this ability cannot be used more than once per check.  
- **Keen:** **Glimpse** now costs **1 RP**.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Alert:** **Vigilance** now also alerts any targets of your choice within 100ft, making them aware of this combat.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passives:**  
- **Hyperfocus:** **Focus** can now be used repeatedly on the same check as long as the new roll is still under half of your die’s maximum.  
- **Resolute:** **Glimpse** can now be used on a target any number of times once they alert you, limited only by your RP.

# **Sight**

*Requires Perception 1, 3, or 5, depending on if your base Sight is Heightened, Primary, or Secondary, respectively.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Eagle Eye:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half your total skill points.*** When you would need to make a perception check to see something, you can use this ability. For that check, distances on the Sight Table count as one step closer (1,000ft → 100ft → Melee), and you can make out fine details (such as faces, writing, or insignia) at up to 1,000ft. Can only be used once per instance.

  **Tier 2: You unlock the following action:**

- **Adjustment:** When in high light or low light conditions where your sight is naturally obscured, you can spend 10 minutes of uninterrupted time to adjust your vision to the situation, letting your eyes adjust as normal in that time instead of the usual hour. Once you leave the condition, this effect ends.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Pinpoint:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half your total skill points.*** When you would need to make a perception check to see something, you can use this ability. Your sight counts as one level higher (Secondary-\> Primary-\> Heightened) for that check. If already Heightened, your check instead gains advantage. Can only be used once per instance. Has no effect on artificially lowered sight levels (such as being blinded).  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Hyper Adjustment:** **Adjustment** now only takes a single minute to accomplish, and maintains even when the light conditions end for up to an hour afterward. This means that if you re-enter those light conditions within the hour, your eyes are still adjusted to the environment.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Fixation:** **Pinpoint** can now be used multiple times per check. Every successive use’s energy cost is 2x higher than the prior use (meaning if you used it three times on one check, it would cost 1x \+ 2x \+ 4x energy cost).

# **Hearing**

*Requires Perception 1, 3, or 5, depending on if your base Hearing is Heightened, Primary, or Secondary, respectively.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Discriminate: Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Any time you would need to make a hearing check to single out a sound that is being obscured by other sounds of similar loudness, you can use this ability. The covering-sound penalty to your Hearing does not apply to that check.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Environmental Attunement:** You can spend 10 minutes getting used to any environment's sounds. While you remain in that environment, your hearing level is considered one level higher towards any abnormal sounds (Secondary-\> Primary-\> Heightened). If already Heightened, you instead get advantage on any checks made against those sounds. Leaving the environment removes this effect.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Recover: Cost: *Energy equal to half your total skill points.*** You can spend **2 AP** to use this ability to end Deafened, or any effect lowering your Hearing level, so long as its source has ended.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Audio Bias:** **Discriminate** now also grants advantage on that perception check.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Echolocation:** By making clicks or other medium noises (which count as soft noises instead while you are Stealthing), you can use your Hearing in place of your Sight to perceive targets within 100ft, ignoring anything that would obscure your Sight (such as darkness or being Blinded), though you cannot make out purely visual details like color or writing. In addition, **Environmental Attunement** now only takes 1 minute to accomplish, and persists for a week after you adjust to a specific environment.

# **Smell**

*Requires Perception 1, 3, or 5, depending on if your base Smell is Heightened, Primary, or Secondary, respectively.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Stench Trails:** When you successfully smell something, you can now determine its origin point and/or direction.

  **Tier 2: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Filth Normalization:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half your total skill points.*** While among an overpowering odor, you can use this ability to mentally blot it out for just this round. Can be maintained by spending energy every round on this ability, allowing for smell checks to go unbothered.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Visualize: Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** When you use this ability, declare or describe a smell that your character is searching for. Make a smell perception check attempting to find that smell in the area, with your smell level being counted as one level higher for that check.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Bloodhound:** **Filth Normalization** can now be used to block out any smells that you choose, not just terrible ones. In addition, when you use **Filth Normalization**, you can use **Visualize** on the target for no extra energy cost.

  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Smell-O-Vision:** Your character can now “see” the stench trails of targets you have **Visualized**, allowing you to alternatively use sight checks and skills to follow them.

# **Stealth**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Stealthing:** On your turns (whether in or out of combat), you can choose to begin acting stealthily. Doing so causes your movement to be affected as if you were in rough terrain (+1 AP per movement). While stealthing, the volume of sounds you produce is treated as soft instead of medium, and you count as lightly obscured except in well lit environments. Whenever you would be targeted with a Spot, you will need to make a stealth check against it.  
- **Stealth Value:** Your stealth value for each target you would interact with while stealthed can either be “half” or “full”, depending on whether or not one or both of the following are active (if only one is active, you are in half stealth to that character, and if both are active you are in full stealth):  
* The target is unable to sense you, or if they can, they have not spotted you (assuming you are trying to hide from them).  
* The target is unaware of combat occurring. This will always be inactive during combat.  
- **Sneak Attack:** Attacking from stealth can either be done from half stealth or full stealth. Half stealth gives you Advantage on the attack roll, and the target(s) Disadvantage on their dodge roll. Full stealth completely prevents the target(s) from reacting, meaning they roll no dodge roll. Your attack roll is instead rolled to determine if you crit, requiring a roll equal to or higher than the size of one of their dodge dice. If you roll double or more than this value, you instead deal a Double Crit.  
- **Attacking from Stealth:** When you make an attack while Stealthing, the target becomes aware of combat if it was not already. The attack does not reveal you on its own: if one of the target’s senses would Auto Pass against you, it automatically spots you, and otherwise it does not. Either way, your attack alerts the target, allowing it to use **Glimpse** against you this round. Other creatures that perceive the attack also become aware of combat and are alerted by you in the same way, though they must still Spot or **Glimpse** you to find you.


| Stealth States |  |  |
| ----- | :---- | :---- |
| **State** | **What It Means** | **What It Affects** |
| **Stealthing** | You are actively hiding, following the Stealthing rules above. | Required for most stealth abilities, and lets you have a Stealth Value against each target. |
| **Spotted** | A target has found you, through Spot, Glimpse, or by Auto Passing against you. | Removes the first condition of your Stealth Value against that target. |
| **Aware** | A target knows combat is occurring. Every target is aware once combat has begun, unless an ability says otherwise. | Removes the second condition of your Stealth Value against that target. |
| **Alerted** | Something you did this round drew a target’s attention to you, such as attacking it. | Lets that target use Glimpse against you this round. |
| **Surprised** | A character was unaware of combat when it began. | It takes no turn in round 0, and has no RP until its first turn (see Surprise in Initiative). |


  **Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Fade:** You can spend **2 RP** to begin Stealthing outside of your turn.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Steady Nerves:** When a target uses **Glimpse** against you, your stealth check against it has Advantage.

  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability and passive:**

- **Still Breath:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When the result of a stealth check you make would be less than half of your die’s maximum, you can use this ability to reroll the check. You must take the new result, and this ability cannot be used more than once per check.  
- **Practiced:** **Fade** now costs **1 RP**.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Hush:** When you use **Fade**, allies of your choice within 100ft who can sense you may each spend **1 RP** to also begin Stealthing.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following action and passive:**  
- **Shake Off:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per turn, you can spend **2 AP** to force every target that has spotted you to make a new perception check against your stealth check. Each that fails no longer counts as having spotted you. A target whose sense would Auto Pass against you on that sense’s table makes no check, as it can already perceive you, and continues to have you spotted.  
- **Deep Breath:** **Still Breath** can now be used repeatedly on the same check as long as the new roll is still under half of your die’s maximum.

# **Concealment**

*Requires Stealth 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Hunker Down:** While Stealthing, you can spend **1 AP** to stay still. Until you move or take an action, you count as one step more obscured (unobscured → lightly → heavily), meaning you are lightly obscured in well lit environments and heavily obscured everywhere else.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Stash:** You can hide an object on your person or in your surroundings. This takes **2 AP** for an object you can hold in one hand, or 1 minute for anything larger (up to roughly your own size). Make a stealth check and note the result. Anyone searching for the object must beat that result with a perception check, and the result holds until the object is moved.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Blend In:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** While within a crowd, you can spend **1 AP** to begin Stealthing, even while spotted. Until you leave the crowd, targets’ Hearing and Smell count as one level lower against you, and you count as heavily obscured to any target more than 100ft away.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Deep Cover:** While **Hunker Down** is active, a target that fails a perception check against you cannot attempt to perceive you again until you move or take an action.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following action and passive:**  
- **Hideout:** You can spend 10 minutes preparing a hiding spot large enough for you and up to 5 allies, so long as your surroundings reasonably allow for one (such as furniture, foliage, rubble, or other things to work with; a bare, empty room offers nothing to build a hideout from). While hidden there, those allies use your stealth check in place of their own, and gain the benefits of **Hunker Down** while they stay still.  
- **False Bottom:** Objects you **Stash** on your person can only be found by a search that physically touches you, and that search’s perception check is made with Disadvantage.

# **Sleight**

*Requires Stealth 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Lift:** You can spend **2 AP** to take a hand-sized, unsecured object (such as one in a pocket, on a belt, or lying on a table) from a target within melee. Make a stealth check against the target’s perception check, as well as against any other observer who could perceive the act. You do not need to be Stealthing to do this. Any that succeed notice the attempt.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Plant:** **Lift** can instead place a hand-sized object on the target, or swap an object you are holding for the one you take.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Deft:** **Lift** now costs **1 AP**, and can take secured objects (such as a tied pouch or a sheathed weapon), though not anything held or worn as armor.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Quick Hands:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When a target would succeed on a check to spot you, you can use this ability to take one action costing **1 AP** or less (such as pocketing, dropping, drawing, or using an item) before you count as spotted.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Snatch:** **Lift** can now take an object the target is holding, regardless of its size, as long as you have a free hand. During combat, once per turn, you can instead spend **2 AP** and ***Energy equal to your total skill points*** to make an attack roll against the target in place of the stealth check. On a hit, you deal no damage and take the object instead.

# **Infiltration**

*Requires Stealth 2\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Pick:** You can spend **2 AP** to attempt to open a lock or disable a trap you are aware of. Make a stealth check against a static target set by the GM (Easy through Legendary). On a failure, the mechanism stays shut or armed, and you make a medium noise. The GM decides whether tools are needed. Without this action, locks can only be opened by other means (such as their key, force, or magic), and traps can only be avoided or set off.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Case the Joint:** You can spend 10 minutes observing a location from outside. You learn its visible entry points, the patrol patterns of the guards you observed, and the static target of any lock or trap you can see. For the next hour, your stealth checks against those guards have Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Soft Touch:** **Pick** now costs **1 AP**, and failing no longer makes a noise.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Ghost Step:** While Stealthing, your movement no longer costs the extra **1 AP**.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Slip Past:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** While Stealthing, you can use this at the start of your turn. Until the end of your turn, targets that have not spotted you cannot Auto Pass against you, and must make a perception check instead, even at melee range. Taking any action other than movement ends this early.

# **Ambush**

*Requires Stealth 3\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Lie in Wait:** While Stealthing, you can spend **1 AP** to choose an attack, a target that has not spotted you, and a trigger (such as “when the guard opens the door”). When the trigger occurs, you can spend RP equal to that attack’s AP cost to make it as a reaction attack at no additional cost, using your current Stealth Value. The readied attack lasts until it is used, you are spotted, or you stop Stealthing. You can only have one readied attack at a time.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Quiet Work:** When you attack from stealth, other creatures that perceive the attack still become aware of combat, but are not alerted by you, meaning they cannot use **Glimpse** against you because of it. Only the target is alerted.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Vital Point:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** When you make a Sneak Attack, you can use this ability. If the attack hits, it counts as one step higher: a hit becomes a crit, and a crit becomes a Double Crit.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Coordinated Strike:** When your Sneak Attack starts combat, up to two allies within 100ft who are Stealthing and have not been spotted by the target can each spend RP equal to the AP cost of an attack of their choosing to make it against that target as a reaction attack at no additional cost, during round 0, right after that attack resolves. Each ally uses their own Stealth Value.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Blindside:** During combat, a target that has not spotted you and has not been attacked by you at any point during that combat counts as unaware of combat for your Stealth Value. Once a target has spotted you or been attacked by you, this no longer applies to it for the rest of that combat, even if you hide from it again or leave and return. This only resets once that combat ends.

# **Initiative**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Initiative:** At the start of combat, all characters, whether they are surprised or not, make an initiative check. Turns then go from the highest initiative to the lowest, using the same scores every round (see Turn Order in the Rules). If two or more characters tie, they reroll against each other to settle their order.  
- **Surprise:** When combat begins, any character that was unaware of it is surprised. If at least one character is surprised, combat opens with a surprise round (round 0). In it, each side with at least one non-surprised character chooses one of them as its opener. The openers each take a full turn, in initiative order, and everyone else waits for round 1\. If a character’s action is what starts combat (such as a Sneak Attack), they must be their side’s opener, and that action begins their round 0 turn. Surprised characters have no RP during round 0, and only gain it at the start of their first turn. Non-surprised characters keep any RP they had going in, and refill it when round 1 starts.  
- **Delaying:** As long as your turn has not started yet (including at the start of a round, even if you are first in the order), you can choose to lower your initiative to any score below its current one. You keep that new score for the rest of combat, and take your turn when it comes up in the order.  
    
  **Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Alacrity:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When you make an initiative check, you can use this ability to roll it with Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Poised:** While you are not surprised, you can spend up to **2 RP** during round 0 to move, treating each RP as 1 AP of movement, even if you are not your side’s opener. In addition, you win initiative ties instead of rerolling (if more than one tied character has Poised, those characters reroll against each other).  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Collected:** If you are surprised, you still gain your RP when round 1 starts, as if you were not surprised.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Rally:** When you make an initiative check, allies of your choice within 100ft who can sense you can each spend ***Energy equal to half of their total skill points*** to use your result in place of their own. They act right after you, in an order you choose.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive and ability:**  
- **Lightning Reflexes:** **Alacrity** now grants two stacks of Advantage.  
- **Unshakable:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When an effect would lower your initiative, or make you skip or lose your turn, you can use this ability to make an initiative check against its user’s. On a success, it does not apply.

# **Tempo**

*Requires Initiative 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Rebound:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** At the start of a round, if your initiative is lower than your original initiative check, you can use this ability to return it to that original result.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Quickening:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** At the start of a round, you can use this ability to reroll your initiative check. You only use the new result if it is higher, and it lasts for the rest of combat.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Surge:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** At the end of your turn, you can use this ability to carry up to **3 AP** you did not spend into your next turn (meaning those AP go unused on the turn you use this). You cannot use this on two turns in a row.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Seize:** When you use **Quickening**, you can instead spend twice its energy cost to set your initiative to one higher than any character of your choice.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Double Time:** **Cost: *Energy equal to twice your total skill points.*** Once per combat, at the end of a turn in which you acted first in the round, you can use this ability to take an extra turn at the end of that round. This cannot be used during round 0\.

# **Punish**

*Requires Initiative 2\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Exploit Opening:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per turn, when you hit a target that has not taken its turn yet this round, you can use this ability to give that attack’s damage one stack of Strengthened.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Anticipate:** When a target with lower initiative than you attacks you, if you have already hit it this round, your dodge roll against it has Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Punishing Strike:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per round, when a target with lower initiative than you makes an attack against you, or against an ally in melee range of you, you can use this ability. Spend RP equal to the AP cost of an attack of your choosing to make that attack against the target as a reaction attack at no additional cost, resolving before the target’s attack.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Expose:** You can use **Exploit Opening** when an ally hits a target, as well as when you do, paying its energy cost yourself.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Overwhelm:** **Cost: *Energy equal to twice your total skill points.*** Once per round, when you hit a target with lower initiative than you, you can use this ability. Until the start of your next turn, its attack rolls and Non Stat Checks have Disadvantage.

# **Delay**

*Requires Initiative 3\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Stall:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per turn, when you hit a target, you can use this ability to lower its initiative by a quarter of your total skill points (rounded down, to a minimum of 0\) for the rest of combat.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Siphon:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When you lower a target’s initiative with **Stall**, you can use this ability to raise your own initiative by the same amount. This can never give you a second turn in the same round.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Staggering Blow:** When you crit a target, you can use **Stall** on it even if you have already used it this turn, still paying its energy cost.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Bottomed Out:** A target with 0 initiative has Disadvantage on its dodge rolls against you.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Reset:** **Cost: *Energy equal to twice your total skill points.*** Once per round, when you hit a target with 0 initiative that has not taken its turn yet this round, you can use this ability. Its initiative becomes one higher than the highest initiative in the combat, and it does not take its turn this round.

# **Persuasion**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following action and rule:**

- **Persuade:** During roleplay, you can attempt to persuade a target. Doing so requires a persuasion check, going up against a counter check by the target (though this rarely gets any bonuses to it). If the target is friendly, both checks are made normally. If the target is neutral, their counter check has Advantage. If the target is hostile, they cannot be persuaded.  
- **Outcomes:** A successful persuasion makes the target open to what you are asking, but what that means is entirely up to the GM, and it never forces the target to act against its own nature. If you fail, you cannot make the same persuasion check again unless the GM permits it, the situation meaningfully changes (such as you learning something new that supports your case), or you are persuading the target of something different.  
    
  **Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Rapport:** You can spend 10 minutes in conversation with a neutral target, then make a persuasion check against its counter check (made normally, without the neutral Advantage). On a success, it counts as friendly for your persuasion checks for the rest of the day. This only works on targets the GM approves.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Charismatic:** Your persuasion checks against friendly targets have Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability and passive:**  
- **Rephrase:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When the result of a persuasion check you make would be less than half of your die’s maximum, you can use this ability to reroll the check. You must take the new result, and this ability cannot be used more than once per check.  
- **Quick Study:** **Rapport** now takes 1 minute.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Back Me Up:** When you make a persuasion check, one ally taking part in the conversation can make one alongside you. You use the higher of the two results.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passives:**  
- **Lasting Impression:** Targets you have successfully persuaded count as friendly for your future persuasion checks, until you give them reason not to. The GM can still veto this for any target.  
- **Silver Tongue:** **Rephrase** can now be used repeatedly on the same check as long as the new roll is still under half of your die’s maximum.

# **Barter**

*Requires Persuasion 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Haggle:** When buying or selling, you can make a persuasion check against the merchant’s counter check. On a success, the price moves 10% in your favor, or 20% if your result is double theirs or more. On a failure, the price stays where it was.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Read the Deal:** You can spend 1 minute talking with someone you are trading with, then make a persuasion check against their deception check. On a success, you learn whether the price they are offering is fair, and whether they know they are selling a fake or misrepresenting the item.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Repeat Customer:** After you successfully **Haggle** with a merchant, your future trades with them start at that improved price without a new check. This does not stack: you cannot **Haggle** with that merchant again to improve the price further, unless your saved price came from a regular success, in which case you can **Haggle** with them once more to try for the improved result. The GM can veto this for any merchant, and it ends if you give them reason to stop.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Connections:** Once per settlement each week, you can spend an hour asking around for a buyer or seller of a specific item. If one plausibly exists there (as the GM decides), you find them. When the GM rolls that item’s scarcity in that settlement, they roll it with Advantage, and if they have already rolled it there, they reroll it with Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Shrewd Dealer:** **Haggle** now moves the price by 20%, or by 40% if your result is double theirs or more.

# **Diplomacy**

*Requires Persuasion 2\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Parley:** During combat, you can spend **2 AP** to call out to a hostile target, making a persuasion check against its counter check, which it makes with Advantage. On a success, you get a brief moment (no more than a couple of sentences) to get a message across, and it is heard. Combat continues around you as normal, and what the message accomplishes, if anything, is up to the GM. If this fails, you cannot use it on that target again for the rest of the combat. This only works on targets the GM approves, and the target must be able to understand you.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Speak for the Group:** When you persuade a group (such as a crowd, a council, or a squad of guards), you make one persuasion check against the highest counter check among them, or their leader’s, instead of persuading each of them separately.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Common Ground:** After a successful **Parley**, you can immediately make another persuasion check against the target’s counter check (made normally) to persuade it to stop attacking you. On a success, it stops attacking you and counts as neutral toward you, as long as you do not attack it.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Envoy:** When you attempt to improve your party’s relations with a faction or organization, any check you make to do so has Advantage, as long as you have a valid reason for the attempt (such as completing work for them or bringing them something they need), as the GM decides.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Ceasefire:** **Cost: *Energy equal to twice your total skill points.*** Once per combat, you can spend **3 AP** to call for a ceasefire with a hostile group, making a persuasion check against the highest counter check among them, which is made with Advantage. On a success, the whole group stops attacking your party and counts as neutral toward you, as long as no one in your party attacks them. This only works on groups the GM approves, and the group must be able to understand you.

# **Command**

*Requires Persuasion 3\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Intimidate:** Outside of combat, you can make a persuasion check against a target’s counter check, regardless of its attitude toward you (unlike Persuade, this works on hostile targets, whose counter check is made normally). On a success, the target complies with one demand while it feels threatened, and afterward its attitude toward you worsens by one step, as the GM decides. This only works on targets the GM approves.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Rallying Cry:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per round, you can spend **2 AP** so that allies within 100ft who can hear you end any Fear affecting them, and have Advantage on checks to resist Fear until the start of your next turn.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Menace:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per turn during combat, you can spend **2 AP** to intimidate a target within 100ft that can hear and understand you, choosing one of the following.   
  - **Demand:** make a persuasion check against its counter check, which it makes with Advantage. On a success, it must spend its next action on one of your choosing (such as retreating, dropping its weapon, or surrendering), if the GM permits. If this fails, you cannot Demand that target again for the rest of the combat.   
  - **Threaten:** it must resist Fear (as described in the Fear rules) or be afraid until the end of its next turn.


  **Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Demoralize:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per round, when you crit or defeat an enemy, you can use this ability. Other enemies within 100ft who saw it and share an allegiance with it must resist Fear (as described in the Fear rules, against your skill points multiplied by your size) or be afraid until the end of their next turn.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Orders:** **Cost: *Energy equal to twice your total skill points.*** Once per round, you can spend **2 AP** to give an order to an ally within 100ft who can hear you. That ally can immediately spend RP equal to the AP cost of an action of their choosing to take it as a reaction at no additional cost.

# **Deception**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following action and rule:**

- **Deceive:** During roleplay, you can attempt to deceive a target. Doing so requires a deception check, going up against a counter check by the target (though this rarely gets any bonuses to it). If the target is friendly or neutral, both checks are made normally. If the target is hostile, their counter check has Advantage.  
- **Outcomes:** A successful deception makes the target believe what you are telling them, but how they act on it is entirely up to the GM. If you fail, you cannot make the same deception check again unless the GM permits it, the situation meaningfully changes, or you are deceiving the target about something different. GMs are encouraged (but not required) to worsen a target’s attitude toward you by one step (friendly → neutral → hostile) when a deception fails or is later uncovered.  
    
  **Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Cover Story:** You can spend 1 minute preparing to lie. Your next deception check has Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Unreadable:** Hostile targets’ counter checks against your deception no longer have Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability and passive:**  
- **Double Down:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When the result of a deception check you make would be less than half of your die’s maximum, you can use this ability to reroll the check. You must take the new result, and this ability cannot be used more than once per check.  
- **Save Face:** When a deception check made with **Cover Story**’s Advantage fails, you can make a new deception check against a static target equal to half of the target’s counter check result (rounded down). On a success, you save face: the lie still fails, but you suffer no further consequences from it (such as the target becoming suspicious of you or its attitude toward you worsening).  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Play Along:** When you make a deception check, one ally taking part in the conversation can make one alongside you. You use the higher of the two results, but if both fail, the GM may treat the lie as uncovered.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passives:**  
- **Practiced Liar:** Once per hour, you can use **Save Face** on any failed deception check, not only ones made with **Cover Story**’s Advantage.  
- **Straight Face:** **Double Down** can now be used repeatedly on the same check as long as the new roll is still under half of your die’s maximum.

# **Diversion**

*Requires Deception 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Distract:** You can spend **2 AP** (or a moment, outside of combat) to draw the attention of a target in melee range of you, making a deception check against its counter check. On a success, its perception checks against creatures other than you that it does not currently sense have Disadvantage, and it must immediately remake its perception check (with that Disadvantage) against each creature other than you that it has spotted but that its senses would not Auto Pass against, no longer counting as having spotted any it fails against. In combat, this lasts until the end of your next turn. Outside of combat, it lasts as long as you keep its attention (about a minute, or longer if the GM allows).  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Feint:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per turn, you can use this ability to make a deception check against a target’s counter check. On a success, its next dodge roll before the start of your next turn has Disadvantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Look Over There:** You can spend **3 AP** to draw the attention of a target within 100ft that can perceive you away from yourself and others. This works like **Distract**, except that its Disadvantage and remade checks apply to all creatures, including you.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following action:**  
- **False Trail:** You can spend 10 minutes laying a false trail. Anyone tracking you or your party (by sight, sound, or scent) follows the false trail instead, unless their perception check beats your deception check, which you make with advantage.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Smoke and Mirrors:** You can spend **6 AP** to cause a commotion that draws every creature of your choice within 100ft that can perceive it. Make one deception check against the highest counter check among them. On a success, each of them is affected as if by **Look Over There**.

# **Disguise**

*Requires Deception 2\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Disguise:** You can spend 10 minutes, with suitable clothing and materials, to disguise yourself as a general type of person (such as a guard, a merchant, or a noble), though not as a specific individual, and you cannot change your size. The first time a creature has reason to scrutinize you, it makes a perception check against your deception check, seeing through the disguise on a success. After that, it only checks again if you give it a new reason to, as the GM decides. The disguise lasts until you remove it.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Act the Part:** While disguised, your deception checks for lies that fit your disguise have Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Costume Change:** **Disguise** now takes 1 minute. In addition, you can keep one prepared disguise on hand and put it on for **2 AP** (such as in the middle of an escape).  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Impersonate:** You can use **Disguise** to disguise yourself as a specific person you have studied, either by observing them for at least an hour or by having detailed information about them. Creatures who know that person well make their perception checks against you with Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Master of Faces:** You can now use **Disguise** on up to 5 allies as well as yourself, taking the same amount of time. While their disguises hold, they use your deception check in place of their own.

# **Con**

*Requires Deception 3\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Forgery:** You can spend 1 hour with suitable materials to forge a document, seal, or other mundane item. Make a deception check and note the result. Anyone examining the forgery must beat that result with a perception check to tell that it is fake, and the result holds until the forgery is altered.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Hook:** After you successfully deceive a target, your deception checks against it that build on that same lie have Advantage while the lie holds. The GM can veto this for any target.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following action:**  
- **False Identity:** You can spend a day establishing a false identity in a settlement (a name, a history, and people who will vouch for it). Make a deception check and note the result. Anyone there who looks into your background must beat that result with a perception check to find the truth. You can only maintain one false identity at a time.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Plant:** You can spend an hour setting up false information for a target or group to discover (such as a letter left to be found, a rumor, or a staged scene). Make a deception check and note the result. Anyone who comes across it believes it unless they beat that result with a perception check, though how they act on it is up to the GM.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Grand Scheme:** You can spend at least three days planning a con against a specific target or organization. When you carry it out, you and up to 5 allies involved in it use your deception check, with Advantage, for every deception check made as part of the scheme. This lasts until the scheme is finished or falls apart.


  # **Endurance**

  **Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Rest:** Characters must rest 8 hours each day. You can go without rest for up to 3 days, but the amount of rest you’d need to recover is cumulative. After 1 day, you have Disadvantage on all rolls. At 3 days, you fall unconscious until a full 24 hours of rest is completed.  
- **Health Points:** Health Points (HP) are a measure of how beat up and broken your character is. Your HP is determined by (Con+Will+Build)\*(Size\*3), and you regain HP equal to your Con, Will, and Build minimums combined every time you complete an 8hr rest. If your HP drops to 0 or below, you die, and any excess damage is tracked as Overkill damage. Overkill damage is tracked up to your max HP; at that point, your body is destroyed. In addition, one can also lose Max HP, representing actual chunks of your body being taken off. Natural regeneration does not heal Max HP.  
- **Pain Threshold:** Your Pain Threshold is a value that shows when your character would fall unconscious if their current HP dropped below that value, staying unconscious until they rise back above that value. Your Pain Threshold is always equal to half of your Maximum HP, rounded down.  
- **Energy:** Energy is used by all Archetypes to use various abilities and actions. Your maximum energy is equal to 5x your skill points, and you can spend 1 AP to recover 1/10th of your max energy, rounded down. In addition, you regain energy equal to 2 AP of recovery at the start of each of your turns. Outside of combat, your energy is always full. However, during tense scenes outside of combat (such as sneaking past guards, or escaping a collapsing building), energy does not automatically refill, and is instead spent and regained just as it is in combat, with the GM deciding when each turn passes.  
- **Environments:** Every region is a Lush, Fertile, or Extreme biome (the same biome types used by the Gathering Professions). Lush and Fertile biomes impose nothing on their own.  
- **Extreme Biomes:** Harsh places such as deserts, tundra, high peaks, deep jungle, and blighted wastes. While you are in one without shelter, your Athletics checks have Disadvantage, and resting restores only half of the usual HP.  
- **Hazards:** The GM can mark severe conditions in any biome as a Hazard (such as a blizzard, a sandstorm, thin air at the peaks, the Firelands, or the curse of the Wastes). For each hour you are exposed to a Hazard without shelter, you lose health equal to 1/10th of your Max HP, rounded down.  
- **Shelter:** A proper camp, building, or cave removes these penalties while you are in it, as the GM decides.

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Tireless:** You can go one extra day without rest before suffering its effects (having Disadvantage after 2 days instead of 1, and falling unconscious at 4 days instead of 3).  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Forced March:** When your party travels at a Fast pace, you still get 2 hours of downtime instead of none.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability and passive:**  
- **Grit:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When the result of a Constitution, Willpower, or Build check you make would be less than half of your die’s maximum, you can use this ability to reroll the check. You must take the new result, and this ability cannot be used more than once per check.  
- **Hardened:** **Tireless** now gives two extra days instead of one.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Lead the March:** Allies traveling with you count as having **Forced March**. In addition, you are always aware while traveling at a Fast pace.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passives:**  
- **Iron Body:** **Grit** can now be used repeatedly on the same check as long as the new roll is still under half of your die’s maximum.  
- **Endless:** You no longer fall unconscious at the usual point from going without rest. Instead, each further day without rest adds another Disadvantage, up to three, and you fall asleep when you would gain a fourth, staying asleep until you complete the rest you owe.

- **First Aid:** You can spend 10 minutes tending to yourself or another creature. It regains HP equal to half of what it would regain from an 8 hour rest. Each creature can only benefit from this once between rests.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Deep Sleep:** When you complete an 8 hour rest, you regain 50% more HP than normal.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Patch Up:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** During combat, you can spend **3 AP** to tend to a creature in melee range, restoring up to a quarter of what it would regain from an 8 hour rest. This can only restore HP that it lost since the start of your last turn.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Vigil:** You can forgo your own rest to tend to your allies while they sleep. Allies who complete an 8 hour rest within 100ft of you while you do so gain the benefit of **Deep Sleep**. This counts as you going without rest for that day.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Mend:** You can spend 4 hours treating a creature that is at full HP, restoring lost Max HP equal to what it would regain from an 8 hour rest. This does not also restore the HP that comes with that Max HP. Each creature can only benefit from this once between rests.

  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

If you are still below your Pain Threshold at that point, you fall unconscious as normal. You cannot use this again until your HP rises back above your Pain Threshold.

  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

  **Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**

Until the end of your turn, you can use abilities without having enough energy for them, and your energy can drop as low as the negative of your maximum energy. # **Athletics**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Movement:** During their turn, characters can move using 1 AP in any direction they choose for their Movement Speed. Movement Speed itself increments in values of 5ft rounding down, and its value is based on various equipment/abilities that give benefits at the cost of speed, having a minimum of 5ft after all reductions. This is also represented in the game by grid spaces, a grid space equalling 5ft. Base movespeed cannot exceed your size limitations unless you are using specified features to increase it (see size for more info), and is by default equal to your Size’s maximum movespeed. All movement speed percentage effects are additive, so two 20%s result in a 40% decrease. You cannot move through a hostile creature’s space.  
- **Rough/Difficult Terrain:** Rough terrain (swamps, thick brush, rubble) increases movement cost by 1 AP. Difficult terrain (water, steep climbs) increases movement cost by 2 AP. These penalties stack with other forms of movement penalties and slows, but do not stack with each other.  
- **Jumping:** You can jump up to 1/5th of your movement speed vertically, and your full movement speed horizontally. The midpoint of the jump is the jump's peak. Jumping costs 2 AP, and any movement slowing effects are doubly effective on this cost.  
- **Athletics Checks:** Physical feats such as climbing sheer surfaces or swimming against strong currents use an Athletics check, which is a Non Stat Check (a die equal in size to your total skill points).  
- **Climbing:** A surface with handholds counts as difficult terrain. A sheer or slick surface requires an Athletics check when you begin climbing it, against a difficulty set by the GM. On a failure, you fall (see Gravity Force). If you take damage equal to or greater than 1/10th of your Max HP while climbing, or are hit by enough Force to push you, you must spend 3 RP to hold on or begin falling.  
- **Swimming:** Moving against a strong current requires an Athletics check. On a failure, the current moves you instead (GM’s call). You can hold your breath for 10 rounds (1 minute). After that, you lose health equal to 1/10th of your Max HP, rounded down, at the start of each of your turns (or every 6 seconds outside of combat) until you can breathe again.  
- **Carrying:** Every object and creature has a Size, using the same categories as creatures, with Size 0 used for items made for Size 1 creatures. Equipment uses its own Size to determine its power, in place of yours (see Flow State \- Equipment). How much an item counts towards your Load depends on its Size compared to yours:  
* **Two or more Sizes smaller:** 0 Load. The GM may group a large quantity of these into a single item one Size larger.  
* **One Size smaller:** 1 Load.  
* **Your Size:** 6 Load.  
* **Larger than you:** Cannot be lifted, only dragged (+1 AP per move).  
- **Encumbrance:** You can carry up to 6 Load, counting everything held, worn, or packed. While carrying more than 6 Load, you are Encumbered, increasing your movement cost by 1 AP and preventing you from jumping. While carrying more than 12 Load, you cannot move.  
    
  **Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Running Start:** If you have moved at least your full movement speed in a straight line this turn, your next jump this turn travels twice as far, both horizontally and vertically.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Deep Lungs:** You can hold your breath for 30 rounds (3 minutes) instead of 10 rounds (1 minute).  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Push Through:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When you fail an Athletics check, you can use this ability to reroll it. This can be used once per check, and once per turn during combat.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Strong Back:** You can carry up to 8 Load before becoming Encumbered instead of 6, and up to 16 Load before being unable to move instead of 12\.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Peak Condition:** You have Advantage on Athletics checks, and **Push Through** no longer costs energy.

# **Mobility**

*Requires Athletics 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Sure Footing:** Rough terrain no longer increases your movement cost. This only applies to actual terrain, not effects that treat you as if you were in rough terrain (such as Stealthing or crouching).  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passives:**  
- **Climber:** Surfaces with handholds count as rough terrain for you instead of difficult terrain, and you have Advantage on Athletics checks made to climb.  
- **Swimmer:** Water counts as rough terrain for you instead of difficult terrain, and you have Advantage on Athletics checks made against currents.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Scramble:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per turn, on your turn, you can use this ability. Until the end of your turn, terrain no longer increases your movement cost.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Wall Run:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per turn, when you move, you can use this ability to make that move across a vertical surface or the surface of a liquid. If you do not end the move on solid footing, you fall or sink as normal.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Trailblazer:** Allies moving along a path you have traveled since the start of your last turn gain the benefits of **Sure Footing**, **Climber**, and **Swimmer** while on that path.

# **Hauling**

*Requires Athletics 2\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Fireman’s Carry:** Carrying a willing or unconscious creature of your Size counts as 3 Load instead of 6\.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Steady Haul:** Dragging objects or creatures (including creatures you have grappled) no longer increases your movement cost.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Hurl:** Once per turn, when you throw an object you are carrying that is not a Weapon, you can use this ability. The throw counts as at least Average for its throw type, and on a hit you can choose to apply Force to the target equal to the object’s Durability in the direction of the throw instead of dealing damage.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Pack Mule:** While carrying more than 12 Load (16 with **Strong Back**), you are Overburdened instead of being unable to move. While Overburdened, your movement costs \+2 AP instead of \+1, and you cannot use abilities that move you or modify your movement (such as Dash, Leap, Scramble, or Wall Run). You still cannot move while carrying more than 18 Load (24 with **Strong Back**).  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Heavy Lifter:** You can lift objects and creatures one Size larger than you, counting as 12 Load. Equipment one Size larger than you can still be used, but with Disadvantage on checks made with it: armor and shrouds give you Disadvantage on dodge rolls, while weapons and foci give you Disadvantage on attack rolls made with them.

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Breakfall:** Force Damage you take from falling is Weakened.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Kip Up:** The first time you stand up from prone each turn, it costs no AP.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Roll Away:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When you are attacked while prone, you can spend **1 RP** to use this ability.   **Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**

# **Resolve**

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Fear:** Fear can be applied in a variety of ways, but can also occur naturally in situations where your character is experiencing fear, such as being intimidated, experiencing a horror beyond your imagination, coming face to face with a phobia, etc. *To resist the effects of Fear when applied this way, roll Constitution, Willpower, or Build, requiring a result at least equal to the fear source’s Skill Points multiplied by its Size.* If the source of fear is not a creature, the result required is up to the GM. While affected by Fear, a character may not gain Energy from any source, including regen.  
- **Counter Checks:** A counter check is a Non Stat Check (a die equal in size to your total skill points) made to resist persuasion, deception, intimidation, or any ability that calls for one.  
- **Forced Mental Effects:** Any effect that forces your mind to feel, believe, or act a certain way against your will is a forced mental effect, such as Fear, Intimidate, Menace, Charm, and Illusion. When it is unclear whether an effect counts, the GM decides.  
    
  **Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Second Thoughts:** When you fail a counter check during roleplay, you realize afterward, once the moment has passed, that you were pressured or misled. What you realize is up to the GM.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Iron Will:** When you make a check to resist a forced mental effect, your result cannot be lower than a third of your total skill points, rounded down.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Hold Firm:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When the result of a counter check you make would be less than half of your die’s maximum, you can use this ability to reroll the check. You must take the new result, and this ability cannot be used more than once per check.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Snap Out of It:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per turn, at the start of your turn, if a forced mental effect is affecting you, you can use this ability to make a new check to resist it (**Iron Will** applies).  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Unbreakable:** **Iron Will**’s minimum becomes two thirds of your total skill points, rounded down. In addition, **Hold Firm** can now be used repeatedly on the same check as long as the new roll is still under half of your die’s maximum.

# **Courage**

*Requires Resolve 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Brave:** You have Advantage on checks to resist Fear.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Fight Through It:** While afraid, you still regain energy at the start of your turn, but only half of the normal amount.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Face Your Fear:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** When you fail a check to resist Fear, you can use this ability to reroll it against half of the required result, rounded down.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Lead by Example:** When you successfully resist Fear, allies within 100ft who can see you have Advantage on their checks to resist Fear from the same source until the end of the round.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Fearless:** Once per combat, when you would become afraid, you can choose to resist it automatically. In addition, **Fight Through It** now lets you regain your full start-of-turn energy while afraid.

# **Composure**

*Requires Resolve 1\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Level Head:** Your counter checks against intimidation (such as Intimidate and Menace’s Demand) have Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Stubborn:** When a creature you are friendly toward tries to persuade you, your counter check has Advantage, as if you were neutral toward it.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Tight-Lipped:** While you are being questioned or pressured for information and choose not to reveal something, you give away no tells, and any attempt to read whether you are hiding something has Disadvantage.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**  
- **Unflappable:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per turn, when you make a roll with Disadvantage or Weakened from a forced mental effect, you can use this ability to ignore one stack of it on that roll.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Composed:** Once per combat, when a forced mental effect would make you spend AP or RP, move, or take an action you did not choose (such as Menace’s Demand), you can ignore that part of the effect.

# **Insight**

*Requires Resolve 2\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following action:**

- **Read Intent:** You can spend 1 minute observing a creature you are interacting with, then make a counter check against its deception check. On a success, the GM tells you its general emotional state and whether it is hiding something, though not what.  
    
  **Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Eye for Fakes:** Your perception checks to see through disguises, forgeries, false identities, and planted information have Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Call It Out:** When you succeed on a counter check against a deception, allies who were present for the same lie and can hear you can make a new counter check against it, with Advantage.  
    
  **Tier 4: You unlock the following action:**  
- **Profile:** You can spend 10 minutes observing or talking with a creature, then make a counter check against its deception check. On a success, the GM tells you one of the following, your choice: its attitude toward you, what it currently wants, or whether something present worries it. You can only use this on the same creature once per day.  
    
  **Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**  
- **Seen It All:** **Hook** and **Grand Scheme** no longer give Advantage on deception checks made against you.

# **Discipline**

*Requires Resolve 3\.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Focused:** Your counter checks against deception checks made during combat (such as Feint, Distract, Look Over There, and Smoke and Mirrors) have Advantage.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Eyes Forward:** When an effect such as Distract or Look Over There would make you remake perception checks against creatures you have already spotted, you do not remake them. You still have the Disadvantage against creatures you have not spotted yet.

**Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Steady Rhythm:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total skill points.*** Once per round, when your initiative would be lowered, you can use this ability to halve the amount it is lowered by, rounded down.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Shut It Out:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your total skill points.*** Once per turn, at the start of your turn, you can use this ability to choose one enemy ability currently giving you Disadvantage (such as Overwhelm). You ignore one stack of that Disadvantage until the end of your turn.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Unbroken Focus:** Once per combat, when your initiative would be lowered to 0, it is lowered to 1 instead.
