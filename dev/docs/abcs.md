# **ABC Rules** {#abc-rules}

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Ancestry, Background, and Culture:** Every character of high sapience is defined by three choices, made in order. You first pick an Ancestry, then a Background from that Ancestry’s list, then your Culture: one Homeland, from your Ancestry’s list or the Shared Homelands, and one Circle, from your Background’s list or the Shared Circles.  
- **Ancestry:** Your Ancestry is the people you are born to. Each Ancestry has a core tree shared by the wider kind it belongs to (all Wuson-descended peoples share the Wusonian core), usually a lineage tree shared by its line (such as Commonkin for the Human line), and one or more meat trees unique to it, or shared with closely related Ancestries. None of these trees require another. You start with the Tier 0 of each of your Ancestry’s trees, and buy their other tiers as normal.  
- **Background:** Your Background is how you were raised and what you did before the story began. It grants 15 free points of starting skill trees at normal tier costs, including any Theory tiers those trees require. Free points unlock trees and count toward their requirements, but toward nothing else: not dice, not the Stat Bonus, and not Energy. Any points a Background cannot place go into your Ancestry’s trees instead. Some Backgrounds are shared across several Ancestries, with small differences for each.  
- **Culture:** Your Culture has two parts: your Homeland, the people and place you come from, and your Circle, the group you came up in through your Background. Together they set your values, customs, outlook, and ties in the world. Cultures are purely roleplay, and have no mechanical effect.  
- **Sapience:** Every creature sits in one of three bands of sapience. Sapience sets what a creature can draw on; age and experience set how far it has grown within that, adding stat and skill points over time.  
* **High sapience (band 3):** Peoples such as Humans and Orcs. They have full access to Ancestry, Background, and Culture, and start at about 3 stat points per skill point.  
* **Mid sapience (band 2):** Creatures capable of some higher learning, perhaps tool use or social life, but far more limited. They have an Ancestry and a Background but no Culture, meaning no defined cultural roleplay of their own, and they keep the free skill trees their Background grants. Their ratio is about 5:1, sometimes wider.  
* **Low sapience (band 1):** Creatures with no real intelligence, unable to use tools in any meaningful way and hard-pressed to learn tactics. This covers most animals, such as cats, deer, and most dogs. They have only their Ancestry and get no free trees. Their ratio is about 10:1 or worse, widening the lower their sapience.

# **Human** {#human}

**Profile:**

- **Lineage:** One of the two founding races of the Twin Continents, descended from the Wuson. Ancestors of the Dwarves, Halflings, Elves, Dusken, and Drakenblood.  
- **Homeland:** The United States of Tartan (north of the eastern continent), though their explorative, inquisitive nature puts them nearly everywhere.  
- **Size:** 3\.  
- **Height:** 5 ft to 6 ft 4 in.  
- **Weight:** 100 to 250 lb.  
- **Languages:** Common.  
- **Lifespan:** About 80 years.  
- **Backgrounds:** Explorer, Scholar’s Apprentice, Recruit, Apprentice Mage, Novice, Hunter, Trader’s Apprentice, Laborer, Urchin, Outsider.  
- **Homelands:** Guild-Town, Crownland, Old Pantheon, or a Shared Homeland.

# **Namuh** {#namuh}

**Profile:**

- **Lineage:** Humans changed by the Goblin research wing for the Super Soldier Program during the Continental War, which used cancer cells to make soldiers who could regenerate on the battlefield. Most mutated horribly instead, healing worse and living shorter lives, but the rare few who learn to control it become what the Humans wanted. None are made anymore, but their genes are dominant, so their numbers are growing fast. Descended from the Wuson through the Humans.  
- **Homeland:** Human lands, mostly The United States of Tartan, where they live among Humans and blend in easily.  
- **Appearance:** Mostly human, with a few small lumps under the skin, but people tend to sense something slightly off about them that is hard to place. The more a Namuh has mastered their altered body, the harder this is to notice.  
- **Size:** 3\.  
- **Height:** 5 ft to 6 ft 6 in.  
- **Weight:** 110 to 280 lb.  
- **Languages:** Common.  
- **Lifespan:** About 40 years, as their altered bodies break down. Those whose bodies stabilize can live as long as 120\.  
- **Backgrounds:** Explorer, Scholar’s Apprentice, Recruit, Apprentice Mage, Novice, Hunter, Trader’s Apprentice, Laborer, Urchin, Outsider.  
- **Homelands:** Guild-Town, Crownland, Old Pantheon, or a Shared Homeland.

- **Height:** 4 ft 2 in to 5 ft.

- **Languages:** Common (Dwarven dialect, shared with Halflings).  
- **Lifespan:** About 80 years.  
- **Backgrounds:** Engineer’s Apprentice, Mine Hand, Recruit, Apprentice Mage, Novice, Hunter, Trader’s Apprentice, Laborer, Outsider.  
- **Homelands:** Iron Republic, Moon Faithful, Common Folk, or a Shared Homeland.

# **Sworan** {#sworan}

**Profile:**

- **Lineage:** Descended from the Wuson. The first people of the far west, living in the Rust Caves long before the Dwarves arrived. Much of the Dwarven dialect comes from them. The Dwarves enslaved them on landing to build their settlements, and freed them around 1585, after winning the war against the Orcs. Some older Sworan still hate the Dwarves, but most now feel neutral toward them.  
- **Homeland:** The Rust Caves (beneath the Dwarven Republic, south-west of the western continent). Most now live within Dwarven society, though still mostly underground, and few come to the surface unless they need to or have an adventurous streak.  
- **Appearance:** Gray, stone-hard skin and pale eyes. The metals in their bodies pool on their dominant side, stiffening that leg and filling that arm, which has no hand and can be reshaped like the metal it is.  
- **Size:** 3\.  
- **Height: 6 ft 2 in to 7 ft 2 in.**  
- **Weight:** 300 to 500 lb.  
- **Languages:** Sworan. They understand Common (Dwarven dialect) but cannot speak it.  
- **Lifespan:** About 80 years.  
- **Backgrounds:** Engineer’s Apprentice, Mine Hand, Recruit, Apprentice Mage, Novice, Hunter, Laborer, Outsider.  
- **Homelands:** Iron Republic, Moon Faithful, Common Folk, or a Shared Homeland.

# **Wasteling** {#wasteling}

**Profile:**

- **Lineage:** Any. A person of any Ancestry, decayed beyond recognition by about a decade in the Dragon Wastes. Most are Drakenblood or Humans (the Wastes were once Human land, Silver Springs), with some Dwarves, Fire Walkers, and the very rare Goblin. Most people, the Drakenblood above all, pity them as cursed, sorry creatures. Playable Wastelings have left the cult that worships the spirits sealed in Old Draken Hold, or never joined it.  
- **Becoming One:** Pick your original Ancestry as normal, then replace its core tree with the Wasteling tree. You keep its lineage and Ancestry trees.  
- **Homeland:** The Dragon Wastes (south of the western continent). Most never leave, held there by the curse and by whatever brought them there in the first place, living in the cults or the Wastes’ settlements. Playable Wastelings are the rare few who left.  
- **Appearance:** Your original Ancestry, decayed: sloughing skin, exposed sinew, and clouded eyes.  
- **Size and Languages:** As your original Ancestry.  
- **Height:** As your original Ancestry, often an inch or two less from a stooped, decaying frame.  
- **Weight:** Your original Ancestry’s, minus 10 to 20% from the decay.  
- **Lifespan:** Cannot die of natural causes. Most turn feral 20 to 40 years after the change, though some find ways to hold it off. A Wasteling can still have children, who are Wastelings of the same Ancestry, for 10 to 20 years after the change, until they begin to turn.  
- **Backgrounds and Homelands:** Those of your original Ancestry.

# **Wusonian** {#wusonian}

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Size:** Wuson-descended peoples are Size 3, unless their Ancestry says otherwise.  
- **Body:** Upright on two legs, with two arms ending in grasping hands.  
- **Regeneration:** Heals naturally through rest (see Rest in Flow State \- Generic Groups), but cannot regrow lost Max HP or limbs.  
- **Needs:** Breathes air (see Swimming in Flow State \- Generic Groups), and needs food, water, and sleep. Omnivorous.  
- **Senses:** Sight (Primary), Hearing (Primary), and Smell (Secondary) (see Senses in Flow State \- Generic Groups).  
- **Warm-Blooded:** Your body holds its own temperature, so hot or cold biomes that are not Extreme never hinder you, even where they would slow a cold-blooded creature (see Environments in Flow State \- Generic Groups).

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Acclimated:** Choose one biome you were raised in (such as jungle, desert, mountains, sea, or tundra). While you are in it, its Extreme penalties do not apply to you, and you have Advantage on Athletics checks. Living in a different biome for a month changes your choice to that biome.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Hardy Constitution:** Resting in an Extreme biome restores your full HP, and health you lose to Hazards is halved (rounded down).

**Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Shed the Heat:** Once per turn, after you move, you can spend **2 RP** to regain energy as though you had spent 1 AP recovering.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Adaptive Lineage:** **Acclimated** now covers two biomes, and changing one takes a week instead of a month.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Endless Adaptation:** You are **Acclimated** to every biome you have lived in for at least a week, and you do not lose health to Hazards in them.

## **Commonkin** {#commonkin}

*Lineage tree of the Human line: Human, Namuh, Dwarf, Halfling, Elf, Myrthel, Dusken, Dawn Elf, and Drakenblood.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Inquisitive:** When you spend at least 10 minutes examining an unfamiliar object, creature, or place, the GM tells you one useful thing your character could reasonably work out about it.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Watch and Learn:** Once per day, after you see an ally succeed on a Non Stat Check, your next check of the same kind that day has Advantage.

**Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Pass It On:** Once per day, you can spend 10 minutes teaching an ally. Choose one kind of Non Stat Check (such as Athletics or perception checks). Until that ally’s next rest, their checks of that kind cannot be lower than a third of your total skill points, rounded down.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Wanderer’s Sense:** You always know which way is north, you can retrace any route you have traveled in the past month, and you cannot become lost except through magical or similar effects.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Ever Learning:** **Watch and Learn** can now be used once per hour instead of once per day, and **Pass It On** can teach up to 5 allies at once.

## **Bestial** {#bestial}

*Lineage tree of the Orc line: Orc, Fire Walker, Minotaur, Goblin, and Smoblin.*  
**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

# **Lakeborn** {#lakeborn}

Core tree of every creature changed by the Firelands lake.  
**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Size:** Lakeborn keep the Size of the kind they were before the change, unless their Ancestry says otherwise.  
- **Body:** The lake turns each kind of creature into the same new form every time, as its Ancestry describes.  
- **Changed Once:** The change is permanent and happens only once. Drinking from or bathing in the lake again does nothing, and Lakeborn breed true.  
- **Regeneration:** Heals naturally through rest (see Rest in Flow State \- Generic Groups), but cannot regrow lost Max HP or limbs.  
- **Needs:** Breathes air (see Swimming in Flow State \- Generic Groups), and needs food, water, and sleep, unless its Ancestry says otherwise.

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Lake-Kin:** Low-sapience Lakeborn creatures treat you as one of their own, and do not attack you unless you or your allies harm them first (GM’s call).

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Freshwater Born:** You swim at your full movement speed and can hold your breath twice as long (see Swimming in Flow State \- Generic Groups).

**Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Lake Surge:** Once per combat, you can spend **2 AP** to let the lake’s change surge through you. Until the start of your next turn, Stage 1 movement slows do not affect you, and you have Advantage on checks to resist being grappled, moved, or knocked Prone.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Set in Flesh:** You have Advantage on checks to resist effects that would change your body or form against your will.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Endless Tide:** **Lake Surge** can now be used once per round instead of once per combat.

# **Wasteling** {#wasteling-1}

## **Sworan** {#sworan-1}

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Body-Speech:** Your vocal cords are primitive and cannot form the sounds of other languages. You speak Sworan, a language of clicks, knocks, and guttural noises made with your whole body. You understand other languages as normal, and can still use hand-sign, writing, or gesture.  
- **Living Metal Arm:** Your dominant arm is living metal, with no hand. Over 1 minute, you can reshape it into any Foci, any Icon Form, a Weapon of any type and weight, or back into a plain arm. As a Foci or Icon, it counts as Attuned for as long as it holds that shape. As a Weapon, it must be made of a material you have absorbed, starting with Iron. To absorb a Common Weapon material, spend an 8 hour rest merging an item made of it (or enough of the raw material for one) into your arm. The item is used up, but you can use that material from then on without it. As a Weapon it uses its material’s Durability and Limit, and a Heavy-only material uses half of them as a Light Weapon. Its grade is always high enough for all of your Scaling Stat to count, and you cannot be disarmed of it.  
- **Deep-Dweller:** Hearing is Heightened for you instead of Primary, and darkness only lightly obscures your Sight instead of heavily.

**Tier 1: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Mimicry:** Once per round, you can spend **1 AP** to mimic a sound you have heard in the past day, such as a voice saying something it said, an animal call, footsteps, or a mechanism. Creatures that hear it take it for the real thing unless they succeed on a perception check of at least your total skill points.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Deepforged:** You can now absorb Uncommon Weapon materials into your **Living Metal Arm**.

**Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Stone Skin:** Physical damage you take from each attack is reduced by your Constitution Min.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Coreforged:** You can now absorb Rare Weapon materials into your **Living Metal Arm**.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Quick Reshape:** Once per round, you can spend **1 AP** to reshape your **Living Metal Arm** instead of taking a minute.

- **Flamekin:** Heat damage you take gets one Weakened stack, and **Searing Zeal** can now be used once per round instead of once per combat.

## **Minotaur** {#minotaur-1}

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Warm-Blooded:** Your body holds its own temperature, so hot or cold biomes that are not Extreme never hinder you, even where they would slow a cold-blooded creature (see Environments in Flow State \- Generic Groups).  
- **Thick Hide:** Your hide is natural Armor (see Martial Armor in Flow State \- Equipment). Over an 8 hour rest, you can grow it out as Light or Medium Armor, or shed it entirely. It has 15 Durability and 3 Limit as Light, 30 and 6 as Medium, 60 and 12 as Heavy, and 120 and 24 as Titanic, with the normal penalties of its weight. It scales with your Constitution as normal, and its grade is always high enough for all of your Constitution to count. It fully repairs whenever you complete a rest. While it is grown out, you cannot wear other Armor.

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Sea Legs:** A ship’s motion never knocks you Prone or makes your footing rough terrain, and storms and rough water do not slow your swimming.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Privateer’s Eye:** You can judge the rough value of any cargo, ship, or loot you can see, and you have Advantage on checks to haggle over plunder or salvage.

**Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Red Tide:** Once per combat, when an attack damages you, you can spend **1 RP** to fly into a rage until the end of your next turn. While raging, your melee attacks are Strengthened, and your dodge rolls have Disadvantage.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Toughened Hide:** You can now grow your **Thick Hide** as Heavy or Titanic Armor.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Unending Rage:** **Red Tide** can now be used once per round instead of once per combat.

## **Goblin** {#goblin-1}

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Unlucky:** Once per rest, when a creature would hit you with an attack, you can force it to reroll the attack and use the lower result.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Magic-Wary:** You have Advantage on your first check each combat to resist the effects of a Spell.

**Tier 3: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Street Smarts:** While in a settlement, you have Advantage on perception checks to notice threats, ambushes, and pickpockets.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Mob Rule:** Once per combat, you can spend **2 AP** to mark a creature within 100ft for your allies who can hear you. Until the start of your next turn, each ally’s first attack against it has Advantage.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Jinx:** **Unlucky** can now be used once per combat instead of once per rest, including when a creature would hit an ally within 30ft of you. If the rerolled attack still hits, its damage gets one Weakened stack.

## **Smoblin** {#smoblin-1}

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following rules:**

- **Awakened Mind:** Changing your Alignment takes a 10 minute activity for you instead of 1 hour.  
- **Loud Mind:** Your mind is unusually loud. Creatures with a Psion sense can detect you from twice its normal range.

**Tier 1: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Steady Focus:** You have Advantage on Willpower checks to keep Concentration.

**Tier 2: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Light Step:** Each point of Alignment gives you a 10% speed penalty instead of 20%.

**Tier 3: You unlock the following ability:**

- **Overclock:** Once per combat, you can spend **1 RP** to raise your Alignment by 1, up to the deepest depth you have unlocked, until the end of your next turn. That extra point adds no speed or AP penalty.

**Tier 4: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Hardened Mind:** You have Advantage on checks to resist forced mental effects.

**Tier 5: You unlock the following passive:**

- **Redline:** **Overclock** can now be used once per round instead of once per combat.

# **Cultures** {#cultures}

*Every character picks one Homeland and one Circle. Homelands are grouped by Ancestry, with a few Shared Homelands open to anyone. Circles are listed by Background, with a few Shared Circles open to anyone. Cultures are purely roleplay; their Ties are hooks for the GM, never guaranteed outcomes.*

## **Shared Homelands** {#shared-homelands}

- **Wanderer:** You grew up on the move, with no single place to call home. You value self-reliance and a light pack, pick up customs wherever you go, and are welcome nearly everywhere but trusted almost nowhere. Ties: a scatter of old acquaintances along the roads.  
- **Displaced:** War, disaster, or persecution drove your family from its home. You value the people who took you in, keep the old customs alive in small ways, and carry a quiet grudge against whatever drove you out. Ties: other refugees from your homeland, and those who sheltered them.  
- **Crossroads-Born:** You were raised in a trade hub where many peoples meet, such as a great port or market city. You value deals, news, and novelty, speak a little of everyone’s slang, and see borders as a matter of price. Ties: dockhands, innkeepers, and traders of many peoples.  
- **Mixed Heritage:** You were raised between two peoples, by parents or guardians of different Ancestries or Cultures. You value belonging and bridge-building, keep customs from both sides, and are never fully claimed by either. Ties: family on both sides, who may not agree about you.

## **Human Homelands** {#human-homelands}

- **Guild-Town:** You grew up in a Human city where the Merchants Guild is the real power. You value contracts, reputation, and steady coin, mark life’s milestones with dues and ledgers, and see the crown as distant and the Guild as near. Ties: Guild clerks, factors, and debtors.  
- **Crownland:** You were raised in the royal heartlands, loyal to the throne. You value duty, order, and the king’s peace, keep royal holidays and oaths, and look on the Guild’s reach with suspicion. Ties: local lords, guard captains, and royal officials.  
- **Old Pantheon:** Your family keeps to the old Human gods that most of your people have drifted away from. You value tradition and the cycle of the seasons, keep household shrines and seasonal rites, and are seen as quaint by city folk. Ties: the last village priests and keepers of old shrines.

## **Dwarf Homelands** {#dwarf-homelands}

- **Iron Republic:** You were raised among the Dwarves who see the Republic’s strength in its army and its arsenal. You value discipline, rank, and victory, mark coming of age with service, and hold the old grudge against the Orcs as a point of pride. Ties: officers, veterans, and arms-works foremen.  
- **Moon Faithful:** Your people worship the moon as the source of magic and believe every gem construct has a mind of its own. You value reverence, restraint, and the dignity of constructs, keep night vigils under the moon, and are distrusted by factory owners. Ties: moon temples, mages, and workers who walked off the line.  
- **Common Folk:** You grew up among the ordinary miners and workers of the Republic, just trying to live. You value hard work, fair pay, and a good council vote, mark the end of a shift with drink and song, and have little patience for factions. Ties: crews, neighbors, and council delegates.

## **Halfling Homelands** {#halfling-homelands}

- **Hills Revelers:** You were raised in the festival towns of Littlefoot Hills, the entertainment heart of the world. You value joy, hospitality, and a good show, keep the revelry gods’ feast days loosely but loudly, and are welcome at any party. Ties: performers, innkeepers, and festival organizers.  
- **Cargo Families:** Your family has moved other peoples’ goods for generations, the middlemen of Human trade. You value reliability, discretion, and a fair cut, keep careful ledgers and route lore, and are trusted by Humans and resented by Goblins. Ties: haulers, warehouse keepers, and trade partners on both ends.  
- **Council-Town:** You grew up in a Halfling town ruled by its own small councils, where real power is local. You value home, neighbors, and having your say, settle disputes over long dinners, and distrust anyone who claims to speak for all Halflings. Ties: councilors, elders, and every family on your street.

## **Elf Homelands** {#elf-homelands}

- **Elven Courts:** You were raised in the manor life of the Elven nobility, where appearance is everything. You value beauty, poise, and standing, dress and speak to be seen, and treat every gathering as a quiet contest. Ties: noble households, tailors, and court gossips.  
- **Paan Faithful:** Your family keeps faith with Paan, who sealed the southern Rift. You value devotion, humility, and gratitude, keep his shrines and sealing-day rites, and are seen as old-fashioned by the fashionable. Ties: temple keepers and the remaining faithful.  
- **Open-City Elves:** You grew up in the open cities of the Paanlands, among markets, magic batteries, and street spellcraft. You value craft, cleverness, and convenience, treat everyday magic like plumbing, and look down a little on those who live without it. Ties: shopkeepers, enchanters, and city guilds.

## **Dusken Homelands** {#dusken-homelands}

- **Paan Faithful:** Your people follow Paan, whose sealing of the southern Rift left the curse you now count as a gift. You value devotion, control of the spirit, and patience, teach the young to master their tether, and see the shadow as a blessing to be earned. Ties: temple elders and spirit teachers.  
- **Tribal Village:** You were raised in a young Dusken village, newly built with old tools. You value community, building, and the tribe’s council, raise homes and halls together, and are proud of how far your people have come. Ties: village elders, builders, and neighboring tribes.  
- **Shadow Supremacist:** You were raised to believe the Dusken curse makes your people superior, and that it should be spread to others. You value strength of spirit, unity, and the shadow’s destiny, keep secret gatherings and oaths, and are feared or scorned by most other peoples. Ties: cells of believers, and enemies among the faithful.

## **Drakenblood Homelands** {#drakenblood-homelands}

- **Reclaimers:** Your family is sworn to retake and restore the Dragon Wastes. You value duty to the lost homeland, endurance, and memory, recite the names of what was lost, and see every other goal as secondary. Ties: Reclaimer camps, veterans of the Wastes, and scholars of the old land.  
- **Wired Cities:** You grew up in Drakenblood cities run on magical wiring, batteries, and waystones. You value precision and understanding how things work, treat magitech as everyday life, and find other peoples’ magic crude. Ties: technicians, scholars, and waystone keepers.  
- **Crown-Sworn:** Your family is sworn to the Drakenblood king and the sorcerer crown. You value loyalty, will, and the bloodline’s strength against temptation, keep courtly oaths, and see the crown as the people’s shield. Ties: royal households, knights, and court mages.

## **Orc Homelands** {#orc-homelands}

- **Great Tribe:** You were born into one of the few large tribes that lead the Wildlands. You value strength, territory, and the nature gods, keep great hunts and seasonal rites, and see the smaller tribes as yours to lead. Ties: chieftains, wildspeakers, and allied Fire Walker clans.  
- **Small Tribe:** You come from one of the many small tribes the great tribes rule by force or bargain. You value survival, kinship, and independence, keep your own small gods and customs, and resent the great tribes as much as you need them. Ties: your tribe, and rivals in the tribe that rules yours.  
- **Borderland:** You were raised on the Wildlands’ frontier with the Dwarves, sworn to the old feud over the coast. You value vigilance, vengeance, and the land itself, keep watch-fires and raid songs, and distrust anyone carrying Dwarven goods. Ties: border warbands and the families of the fallen.

## **Fire Walker Homelands** {#fire-walker-homelands}

- **Guiding Flame Faithful:** Your family keeps the old faith in the Guiding Flame, the lone savior who closed the Fire Walker Rift. You value self-reliance, sacrifice, and helping others, tend small flames as prayers, and see the lake temples as having strayed. Ties: old-faith shrines and wandering faithful.  
- **Lake Temple-Cities:** You were raised in the cities around the Firelands lake, where religion rules and the lake’s changed creatures are revered. You value faith, purity, and order, purify water with flame and prayer, and are wary of outsiders. Ties: priests, temple guards, and the Inquisition’s eyes.  
- **Dirt Clans:** You come from the nomadic clans of the Dirt, its savannas and deserts. You value honor, strength, and tradition, settle disputes by challenge, and measure others by how they endure hardship. Ties: clan elders, rival clans, and the arenas.

## **Minotaur Homelands** {#minotaur-homelands}

- **Red Tusk Horde:** You were raised among the Red Tusks, on the north-east coast or aboard their fleet. You value strength, plunder, and the land that made you, keep the old Guiding Flame, and look down on the Great Flame’s Prophets. Ties: ship captains, Red Tusk generals, and crews that answer to no one.

## **Goblin Homelands** {#goblin-homelands}

- **Family Ward:** You grew up in a part of Tinksmith ruled by one of the five families. You value opportunity, loyalty, and knowing who to pay, treat debts as sacred, and assume everything has a price. Ties: the family that ran your street, and everyone who owes it.  
- **Undercity:** You were raised in Tinksmith’s depths, among abandoned shafts, sewer cults, and towns no one maps. You value secrecy, survival, and getting out, know paths no one else knows, and trust nothing that comes down from above. Ties: tunnel folk, smugglers, and cults best left unnamed.  
- **Island Holdings:** You grew up on one of the Goblins’ island holdings, a research or trading post far from Tinksmith. You value invention, profit, and the sea, live by shipping schedules and company rules, and are a little more open to magic than most Goblins. Ties: company officers, researchers, and ship captains.

## **Shared Circles** {#shared-circles}

- **Self-Made:** You came up on your own, without a mentor or an institution. You value grit and independence, owe nobody, and are owed nothing in return. Ties: few, and all of them earned.  
- **Family Trade:** You were raised into your family’s work alongside parents and siblings. You value the family name and its craft, and inherit its friends, rivals, and debts. Ties: your family and its customers.

## **Background Circles** {#background-circles}

- **Barracks-Raised (Recruit):** You came up through a garrison, drilled alongside other recruits. You value discipline, comradeship, and the chain of command, and still keep barracks habits. Ties: your old unit and its sergeant.  
- **Academy (Apprentice Mage):** You trained at a school of magic or under a tutor’s roof. You value knowledge, practice, and friendly rivalry, and measure people by what they can cast. Ties: tutors, classmates, and rivals.  
- **Temple (Novice):** You were raised within a house of faith, learning its rites from the ground up. You value devotion, service, and humility, and keep the daily observances of your faith. Ties: the clergy and congregation who raised you.  
- **Lodge (Hunter):** You learned among hunters who share game, trails, and stories. You value patience, respect for prey, and pulling your weight, and judge others by how they handle the wild. Ties: lodge members, trappers, and trackers.  
- **Caravan (Trader’s Apprentice):** You came up on the trade roads with a merchant caravan. You value a fair deal, a full wagon, and safe roads, and know every inn between the markets. Ties: caravan masters, guards, and regular buyers.  
- **Workhouse (Laborer):** You worked long shifts among other laborers in a yard, a factory, or a field. You value solidarity, fair pay, and a day’s rest, and stand by your crew. Ties: crewmates and foremen.  
- **Street Crew (Urchin):** You survived the streets with a crew of other children. You value loyalty to the crew, quick wits, and a full belly, and know every alley and hiding place. Ties: your crew, fences, and the watch who chased you.  
- **Expedition Society (Explorer):** You joined a society of explorers who chart unknown lands. You value discovery, preparation, and bragging rights, and keep journals of everything. Ties: expedition leaders, mapmakers, and patrons.  
- **The Stacks (Scholar’s Apprentice):** You learned among archives and libraries, copying and cataloguing. You value accuracy, curiosity, and a good argument, and always know where to look something up. Ties: archivists, scholars, and their patrons.  
- **Construct Works (Engineer’s Apprentice):** You came up in the workshops where gem constructs are built and repaired. You value precision, invention, and working machines, and talk to constructs as if they listen. Ties: engineers, foremen, and the constructs you helped build.  
- **Deep Crews (Mine Hand):** You worked the mines with a crew that trusts each other with their lives. You value safety, steadiness, and the crew above all, and keep the miners’ superstitions. Ties: your crew and its shaft boss.  
- **Traveling Troupe (Street Performer):** You toured with a troupe of performers from town to town. You value the audience, the show, and the troupe, and can make a stage out of anything. Ties: troupe members and patrons in many towns.  
- **The Relay (Courier):** You ran messages and parcels along a courier network. You value speed, discretion, and a delivery made, and know the fastest way between any two places. Ties: relay posts and regular clients.  
- **The College (College Student):** You study at the Elven College of Magic or a school like it. You value scholarship, prestige, and the right connections, and keep the College’s customs and rivalries. Ties: professors, fellow students, and rival students.
