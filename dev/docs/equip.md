# **Martial Weapons** {#martial-weapons}

## **Weapon Info** {#weapon-info}

**Weapon Design:** Weapons are made up of two parts, but can visually be anything that you desire so long as it fits the type of weapon it is and the era your game takes place in. Pick a Weapon type and weight from the list, then a material matching its weight. Weapons with multiple types/materials (but not weights) can exist, but they cost more and are harder to make.  
**Weight:** The weight of a Weapon denotes two things; its Scaling Stat and its AP to attack. Light Weapons scale with Dexterity and take **2 AP** to attack, whilst Heavy Weapons scale with Strength and take **3 AP** to attack (Heavy Weapons generally do more damage and/or have more tags). Weapon weight is set in stone when the material is decided, and cannot be changed without modifying the Weapon. If a material has both weights listed, which one it has is decided when the Weapon is created, and can only be changed by modifying the Weapon.  
**Scaling and Grade:** The damage die count (and other features) listed in each Weapon increases for every 10 points you have in its Scaling Stat, which is Strength or Dexterity depending on the Weight of your Weapon. The grade of a weapon determines the amount a Stat can affect a weapon's scaling, where every 1 grade represents a max of 10 Stat scaling. So, a grade 3 Weapon allows for up to 30 Strength/Dexterity to affect its Stats, any higher would do nothing until the Grade is increased. This also applies to scaling tags such as Knockback, Cleave, etc, limiting them to only allow scaling up to the grade value.  
**One vs Two Hands:** Two handing a Weapon doubles the damage, Knockback, Cleave, Pierce, and Bash of the Weapon. This does not affect any other stats, and does not work with Unarmed Attacks. Changing your held weapon takes **1 AP**, assuming the new one is on your person and easily accessible.  
**Unarmed Attacks:** Unarmed attacks do not have a material but can dynamically shift from being made Light or Heavy. When you go to make one, declare which attack type you are making for that attack. Light Unarmed attacks require **2 AP**, while Heavy require **3 AP.** **Damage dealt to objects from Light unarmed attacks naturally has a stack of Weakened.**

## **Melee Tags** {#melee-tags}

**Broad:** This Weapon’s Durability and Limit are twice as high as they normally would be. **Broad+** gives three times those stats instead.  
**Cleave:** This weapon deals additional damage equal to your Scaling Stat Min against specifically objects, applied before normal damage (still counts for the Limit, so 30 Cleave \+ 30 damage against 30 Limit means the 30 damage goes through while the Cleave hits the armor). **Cleave+** instead deals additional damage to specifically objects equal to your Scaling Stat.  
**Knockback:** Any hit on a target with this Weapon can impart Force to them up to 5x your Scaling Stat in the direction of your attack. All bonus Knockback is additive, and does not change the base. **Knockback+** adds extra knockback equal to the base.  
**Fast:** When you make an attack with a Weapon, you may make another attack at no AP/RP cost with a different held Weapon that has the Fast tag. The extra attack has disadvantage on its attack roll. This triggers once per AP/RP attack. The extra attack has no disadvantage with **Fast+** weapons.  
**Pierce:** This Weapon ignores Limit equal to a quarter of your Scaling Stat. For example, having 5 Pierce value against a Limit 10 object means you would treat it as having only 5 Limit for your attacks. **Pierce+** increases this value to half of your Scaling Stat.  
**Farstrike:** This Weapon’s melee attack range is instead equal to double your normal melee range. **Farstrike+** increases this to quadruple your normal attack range.  
**Solitary:** Once per attack, when you make an attack with a one handed Solitary weapon, you may immediately attack again with disadvantage using no AP/RP. This cannot be done at the same time as an attack with a Fast Weapon. The extra attack has no disadvantage with **Solitary+** weapons.  
**Bash:** When you attempt to attack through an object, if that object has less than or equal Limit than a quarter of your Scaling Stat, this Weapon ignores the Limit of that object and instead applies extra damage to the attack equal to the Limit of that object. Applies once per attack and has no effect when targeting something directly. **Bash+** works on Limit up to half of your Scaling Stat.  
*Note: Bash is not normally present on any Weapons, and is instead activated through skills on certain Weapons, like Balanced and Weighted.*  
**Throwing:** Items can either be considered “good”, “average”, or “bad” when it comes to being thrown. Thrown attacks can use skills that are usable by that Weapon type.  
**Bad:** Has a 50ft range and disadvantage on the attack roll.  
**Average:** Has a 100ft range.  
**Good:** Has a 200ft range and advantage on the attack roll.

## **Ranged Tags** {#ranged-tags}

**Reload:** Ranged Weapons by default need to be reloaded after each shot. This can be modified to be “per X shots” reload types, but those are not covered here.  
	**Slow:** Reloading a slow ranged Weapon takes 3 RP.  
	**Average:** Reloading an average ranged Weapon takes 2 RP.  
	**Fast:** Reloading a fast ranged Weapon takes 1 RP.  
**Range:** This shows the effective range the Weapon can fire within. All ranged Weapons have no direct penalty from firing at melee range, however they are counted as making an attack in melee if this is the case, and the opponent can react accordingly. If you wish to fire at a range higher than listed, you can attempt to fire at double the range with a penalty of a disadvantage on the attack roll, and up to 4x range with a penalty of double disadvantage on the attack roll and a 6 AP attack cost (regardless of weight).  
**Area:** Attacks made with this Weapon can instead be treated as Area attacks. The Area is all targets within 5ft of the targeted position, with the position’s distance being at most half of your Weapon’s range away. If you choose to make the Area attack, your damage rolls for it are Weakened. **Area+** extends the area to be all targets within 10ft of the targeted position, and the position’s range to be equal to the Weapon’s range.  
**Pepper:** Attacks made with this Weapon can fire an additional shot at the same target. A new attack roll is not used for this shot, instead you roll your attack roll with a disadvantage, and on hit both shots hit. As a result, any effects that activate when you attack only trigger once with a pepper attack, but on hit effects trigger twice. **Pepper+** allows for up to 2 bonus shots per attack, each one adding a disadvantage to the attack roll.

**Limit:** How much damage the Weapon can take from each attack it blocks or attacks that are hitting through it. This is ignored if the Weapon is the target of the attack. Only blocks Physical and Elemental damage normally.

# **Martial Armor** {#martial-armor}

## **Armor Info** {#armor-info}

**Armor Design:** Pick an Armor weight from the list, then a material matching its weight. Armors with multiple materials (but not weights) can exist, but they cost more and are harder to make. You can only wear one Armor at a time.  
**Weight:** The weights of Armor have varying effects, as denoted below. Armor weight is set in stone when the material is decided, and cannot be changed without modifying the Armor. If a material has multiple weights listed, which one it is is decided when the Armor is created, and can only be changed by modifying the Armor.  
**Light:** While wearing this armor, your movement speed is 20% lower. Takes a single turn to remove or put on.  
**Medium:** While wearing this armor, your movement speed is 40% lower, and you roll stealth rolls with a disadvantage. Takes a minute to remove or put on.  
**Heavy:** While wearing this armor, your movement speed is 60% lower, and you roll stealth rolls with two disadvantages. Takes 10 minutes to remove or put on.  
**Titanic:** While wearing this armor, your movement speed is 80% lower, and you automatically fail stealth rolls. Takes 1 hour to remove or put on.  
**Scaling and Grade:** The actual stats listed in each Armor increases for every 10 points you have in its Scaling Stat, which is Constitution by default. This is limited by the Grade of the Armor, which denotes how much Stat can actually affect the bonuses of the Armor, where every 1 grade represents a max of 10 Stat scaling. So, a grade 3 Armor allows for up to 30 Constitution to affect its stats, any higher would do nothing until the Grade would be increased.  
**Rarity:** The rarity denoted in each table only affects the scarcity and cost of those items, usually being the following:   
Common \- 1x cost and scarcity of 1 (meaning this is found in 100% of normal stores)  
Uncommon \- 2x cost and scarcity of 0.25   
Rare \- 4x cost  and scarcity of 0.1  
Very Rare \- 8x cost and scarcity of 0.05  
**Durability:** The overall “health” of the Armor. Reaching 0 or lower means the Armor is broken and can no longer be used, but it is still fixable. Negative durability equal to or exceeding the normal max means the Armor is completely destroyed.  
**Limit:** How much damage the Armor can take from each attack it blocks or attacks that are hitting through it. This is ignored if the Armor is the target of the attack. Only blocks Physical and Elemental damage normally.

# **Mental Icons** {#mental-icons}

## **Icon Info** {#icon-info}

**Icon Forms:** Unlike other Archetypal Equipment, a Mental user’s equipment is the Icon, an objection of devotion or ideological belief that takes on a specific named Form. Forms are divided between Dream and Nightmare aligned, and require Attunement to use, taking 6 AP. Only one Form can be Attuned to at a time, but once Attuned a Form does not need to be actively held, so long as it is on your body somewhere (even in a bag or hidden under clothing) it can be utilized to assist in Manifesting/Warding. The Alignment of a Form only dictates what Tenets it is allowed to have, it does not restrict what you can manifest.   
**Scaling and Grade:** The Ward effect listed in each Form increases for every 10 points you have in its Scaling Stat, which is Willpower by default. In addition, the Tenet effect also scales for every 10 points you have in its Wonder’s respective Scaling Stat, be that Ponderance for Dreams or Snappence for Nightmares. These both are limited by the Grade of the Form, which denotes how much Stat can actually affect the Ward and Tenet effects, where every 1 grade represents a max of 10 Stat scaling for each. So, a grade 3 Form allows for up to 30 Willpower, Ponderance, and Snappence to affect its stats, any higher would do nothing until the Grade would be increased.  
**Tenet:** Each Wonder has a specific Tenet effect that is only active if you select it as your desired Tenet when you Attune your Form. Your Form’s Alignment must be the same as the Wonder the Tenet is from. Only one Tenet can be active on a Form at a time, and once unattuned the Tenet dissipates. In addition, you can change the active Tenet by simply spending the Attune cost (by default 6 AP) again.  
**Ward:** The Ward effect is typically an active ability that Forms have so long as you are Attuned to them. By default Wards can only be self-targeting, requiring a self attack as per the rules. Once you have T1 of Mental Theory unlocked, Wards can be Enhanced when they are activated so long as you spend energy equal to your Willpower Min. Unattuning from a Form removes any active Ward effects it has.  
**Rarity:** The rarity denoted in each table only affects the scarcity and cost of those items, usually being the following:   
Common \- 1x cost and scarcity of 1 (meaning this is found in 100% of normal stores)  
Uncommon \- 2x cost and scarcity of 0.25   
Rare \- 4x cost  and scarcity of 0.1  
Very Rare \- 8x cost and scarcity of 0.05  
**Durability and Limit:** Since Forms are intended to be small objects with not much Stats that are often concealed, their Durability and Limit are generally unimportant. All Forms can be treated as having 20 Durability and 4 Limit per Grade (this can be altered by the GM if it makes sense to do so).

# **Magic Foci** {#magic-foci}

## **Foci Info** {#foci-info}

**Foci Design:** Pick a Foci type from among the casting form lists, and pick Affixes if your Foci type can utilize them. Foci cannot have multiple types, are predefined, and only specific Foci have the Multi casting form. To use a Foci, you need to Attune to it by spending one hour magically connecting to it (you can only Attune to one Foci at a time). Once Attuned, you know its location no matter where it is, and while held it can be used to cast your magic. Affix effects and passives for Foci only function if they are Attuned, and they only apply to spells cast through that Foci.  
**Casting Form:** Magic Foci have three different casting forms which dictate passive effects, affix count, how long they take to cast, Scaling Stat, and Threshold Reduction (see Magic for more info). 

1. Igniters are Foci entirely built around having enough power to cast your known spells for you. Mechanically, this means they utilize Grasp as their Scaling Stat and take **2 AP** to cast with, but have 1 Threshold Reduction.   
2. Channelers are Foci that instead have no power themselves, but take your spell concepts and idealize them into reality, so long as they are fed the power to do so. Mechanically, this means they utilize Reach as their Scaling Stat and take **3 AP** to cast with, but have 2 Threshold Reduction.  
3. Foci denoted as Multi Type use Raw Casting AP and TR (1 AP/TR, 2 AP/TR, 3 AP/TR), but use from the lesser of your two Scaling Stats.

**Scaling and Grade:** The Power of spells outputted by your Foci increases for every 10 points you have in its Scaling Stat, which is Reach or Grasp depending on the Casting Form of your Foci. This is limited by the Grade of the Foci, which denotes how much Stat can actually affect the Power of the Foci, where every 1 grade represents a max of 10 Stat scaling. So, a grade 3 Foci allows for up to 30 Reach/Grasp to affect its Power output, any higher would do nothing until the Grade would be increased. Grade also directly impacts the durability and limit of the Foci, independent of your Scaling Stat.  
**Affixes:** Affixes are your weight equivalent for armor, also requiring attunement to either their Offensive or Defensive form. You can have one Affix attuned at no cost, and for every additional Affix attuned your speed is lowered by 20%. This is shared with Shroud’s Affixes.  
**One vs Two Hands:** How many hands hold a Foci or are used to cast a spell has no effect on how the spells perform outside of specific Foci Types. Foci do still count as a held item, however, and cannot be used in the same hand as another held item.  
**Durability:** The overall “health” of the Foci. Reaching 0 or lower means the Foci is broken and can no longer be used, but it is still fixable. Negative durability equal to or exceeding the normal max means the Foci is completely destroyed.  
**Limit:** How much damage the Foci can take from each attack it blocks or attacks that are hitting through it. This is ignored if the Foci is the target of the attack.

# **Magic Shrouds** {#magic-shrouds}

## **Shroud Info** {#shroud-info}

**Shroud Design:** Pick a Shroud type from the list, and pick Affixes if your Shroud type can utilize them. To use a Shroud, you need to Attune to it by spending one hour magically connecting to it (you can only Attune to one Shroud at a time). Once Attuned it becomes melded into your Spirit, meaning its Stats and Affixes are now active on your person, allowing it to protect you as Martial Armor would. Shrouds are considered Magical objects, both while melded and while not melded.  
**Scaling and Grade:** The Stats of your Shroud increases for every 10 points you have in its Scaling Stat, which is Build by default. This is limited by the Grade of the Shroud, which denotes how much Stat can actually affect the Stats and grade of the Shroud, where every 1 grade represents a max of 10 Stat scaling. So, a grade 3 Shroud allows for up to 30 Build to affect its Stats, any higher would do nothing until the Grade would be increased.  
**Affixes:** Affixes are your weight equivalent for armor, also requiring attunement to either their Offensive or Defensive form. You can have one Affix attuned at no cost, and for every additional Affix attuned your speed is lowered by 20%. This is shared with Foci’s Affixes.  
**Shroud Recovery:** Attuned Shrouds naturally recover their spent durability, by default recovering durability equal to its Limit at the start of each of your turns. This can vary, and would be stated in a Shroud’s passive if it is anything else.  
**Durability:** The overall “health” of the Shroud. Reaching 0 or lower means the Shroud is unable to block any more damage, but it can still recover. Shrouds cannot be destroyed unless they are unmelded, or they are directly targeted by arcane damage or similar effects, with durability in the negatives exceeding its normal max resulting in its destruction.  
**Limit:** How much damage the Shroud can take from each attack it blocks or attacks that are hitting through it. This is ignored if the Shroud is the target of the attack. Only blocks Physical, Elemental, and Magical damage normally.

# **Magic Affixes** {#magic-affixes}

**Affixes:** Affixes are different types of Gems (and other crystalline materials) attached to some part of a Foci or Shroud to give it bonuses while attached. Once an Affix is attached to a piece of Attuned equipment, it melds seamlessly into it, and can only be removed by someone who is Attuned to it. While melded, the Affix cannot be targeted by anything. If the melded equipment is destroyed completely, the Affix is destroyed with it. Changing the Affix on a piece of Attuned equipment can be done for **1 AP**.  
**Raw Casting:** An Affix can alternatively be used to enhance Raw Cast spells as per the rules, imparting its effects onto those spells so long as it is held in your hand. Only one Affix can be used for this purpose at a time, unless both hands are used to Raw Cast and each one has a different Affix in it, then both would apply.  
**Rarity:** The rarity denoted in each table only affects the scarcity and cost of those items, usually being the following (note that the scarcity and cost of Affixes are much more expensive and rarer than that of other items due to their innate rarity):   
Common \- 5x cost and scarcity of 0.20 (meaning this is found in 20% of normal stores)  
Uncommon \- 10x cost and scarcity of 0.05   
Rare \- 20x cost  and scarcity of 0.01  
Very Rare \- 40x cost and scarcity of 0.0025  
**Durability and Limit:** Since affixes are intended to be melded into a Foci or Shroud and thus not hittable, their Durability and Limit are generally unimportant. All Affixes can be treated as having 20 Durability and 4 Limit per Grade (this can be altered by the GM if it makes sense to do so).
