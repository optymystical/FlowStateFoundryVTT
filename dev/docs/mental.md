# **Mental Theory** {#mental-theory}

**Tier 0 (Free): You gain the following actions:**

- **Wonders and Modes:** Your Mental power is expressed through Wonders, each Wonder being one end of an Axis of Reality (also called Domains in some settings), these being the skill trees you unlock in Mental. Wonders can either be Dreams (scaling with Ponderance), or Nightmares (scaling with Snappence). Each Wonder has a handful of Modes found in its tiers, which are the individual effects you can Manifest.  
- **Manifesting:** Manifesting a Mode of a Wonder is an attack, made with an attack roll against whatever target is specified. The AP cost of Manifesting is dependent on the Range chosen when Manifesting.  
- **Wonder Power:** The Power of a Wonder increases by 1 for every 10 points you have in its Scaling stat (which is Snappence for Nightmares, and Ponderance for Dreams). All **bolded effects** within the Wonder are increased with its Power, so if you have 30 Snappence and have a Nightmare that deals **1d8 damage**, that would increase to **3d8 damage** due to its power being 3\. If your Scaling Stat is less than 10 or the Power of a Wonder would otherwise be 0, you cannot Manifest it. Any effects that modify the Power of a Wonder are additive, so two 50% increases would result in a 100% increase in Power (rounding down as needed after all power bonuses have been calculated).  
- **Wonder Ranges:** The range of a Wonder is chosen from among the following whenever you Manifest it:

  **Melee:** The Manifestation targets one target within your personal melee range. Has an AP cost of 1\.

  **Ranged:** Has a maximum range of 100ft. The attack is made as normal, respecting cover. Has an AP cost of 2\.

  **Area:** Can either be a 10ft radius around you, a 20ft 90 degree cone in front of you, or a 30ft by 5ft line in one cardinal direction. Each target in the area must make a dodge roll separately against your singular attack roll. Has an AP cost of 3, and the effect is Weakened if it hits more than two targets. This Area can include yourself if desired.

- **Enhancing:** When Manifesting a Mode of a Wonder, you can Enhance it for energy equal to that Wonder’s Scaling Stat min, giving that instance the benefits listed in the Enhance section.  
- **Dismissing Wonders:** You can dismiss concentrated Wonders freely as a reaction. Non-concentrated Wonders you have created can be dismissed by making an attack roll against them (apply Wonder Range AP cost to this). If the target is an object, the rolled number needed to dismiss it is equal to the energy spent on Manifesting it (if 0 then no attack roll is needed), and if it is a creature, it can choose to resist it using its dodge roll, only being dismissed if you successfully hit.  
- **Alignment:** All characters interfacing with the Mental system have an Alignment that they set, which gives various bonuses depending on its position at the cost of speed. Changing your Alignment can only be done with a 1 hour activity. You can choose how far you want to go as desired, gaining all prior bonuses but stacking a 20% speed penalty for each point of Alignment, and Alignment can either be for Dreams or Nightmares.

  **Neutral:** Has no benefits, but you also get no speed penalty.

  **1 Alignment:** Your Dream/Nightmare Form’s Ward has advantage on it’s attack rolls.

  **2 Alignment:** Your Dream/Nightmare Form’s Ward bolded effect is Strengthened.

- **Concentration:** Some abilities and actions in Mental Theory require Concentration to maintain. When you take direct damage while Concentrating on something, you must expend 1 RP to make a Willpower check against the direct damage taken as a target number; if the result is lower, all of your Concentrations end at that moment. You can Concentrate on a number of things equal to your Mental Theory tier, any more must require you losing Concentration on something else. You can end Concentrations freely, and going unconscious removes all active Concentrations.

**Tier 1: You unlock the Psion Arts, Life, Death, Order, Chaos, Beyond, and Below trees. You also gain the following action and passive:**

- **Burst:** You can Manifest a Wonder using RP equal to your chosen Range’s Manifest AP cost if you additionally spend energy equal to its Enhance cost. This can be done in conjunction with Enhance by combining the costs. This also can apply to Form’s Wards and their Enhance Costs.  
- **Defensive Enhance:** You can now Enhance your Form’s Ward, working the same as normal Wonder Modes Enhance actions do.

**Tier 2: You unlock the Ponderance Arts, Creation, Destruction, Peace, War, Adaptation, and Perfection trees. You also gain the following actions:**

- **Trance:** You can spend 8 hours attempting to make permanent a Mode of your choice on a willing target (they must participate in the 8 hours as well). Upon completion, the effect of the Mode is permanently active on them, so long as you maintain Concentration on it (this counts towards your cap).   
  - Modes with a normal lingering effect require a successful self attack roll (crits do apply) at the start of the target’s turns, with a failure meaning it doesn't apply that round.  
  - Modes that have an instanced effect such as Bloom or Decree trigger at the start of each of their turns so long as they make a successful attack roll against themselves (crits do apply), and that effect lasts until the start of their next turn.   
  - Hostile effects are instead treated as a new ability the target can use for an AP cost determined by the Wonder Ranges (as well as the effects those impart), which are chosen on use, using your scaling dice for the effect but their own attack dice/buffs for the attack roll, are automatically Enhanced (if desired) for free, and their own energy if they wish to Burst it.  
- **Project:** Your Form’s Ward can now be applied to an ally as a Ranged attack with a range of 100ft, still requiring whatever AP/RP/energy the Action normally would take.


**Tier 3: You unlock the Snappence Arts, Light, Dark, Earth, and Sea trees. You also gain the following action and passive:**

- **Chant:** If you miss the attack roll on a Manifest, you can, once per Manifest, reroll that attack roll by spending energy equal to its Enhance effect’s cost (this does not Enhance it). The new roll keeps any Disadvantages/Advantages the first attempt had, but does not re-proc effects.  
- **Deepening:** You unlock two new Alignment depths, seen below. While these Alignments are set, you additionally have \-1 AP per turn for each Alignment past 2 (so \-1 AP at Alignment 3, and \-2 AP at Alignment 4).

  **3 Alignment:** Upgrades the 1st Alignment effect to instead be “treat your Dream/Nightmare Form’s Ward attacks as being made from half stealth”, thus giving them advantage on their attack rolls, and targets disadvantage dodging them.

  **4 Alignment:** Your Dream/Nightmare Form’s Ward hits regardless of roll, the roll now being used to determine if a crit occurs.

**Tier 4: You unlock the Willpower Arts, Growth, Stagnation, Fortune, and Ruin trees. You also gain the following actions:**

- **Expansion:** If you expend energy equal to double your Form’s Ward Enhance cost, you can now treat it as an Area attack, which can either be a 10ft radius around you, a 20ft 90 degree cone in front of you, or a 30ft by 5ft line in one cardinal direction.  
- **Fluidity:** You can now spend energy equal to half of your Skill Points multiplied by your current Alignment number to swap your Alignment type.

**Tier 5: You unlock the Esoteric Arts, Truth, Mystery, Zeal, and Serenity trees. You also gain the following passives:**

- **Will of Body and Spirit:** Whenever you make a Martial or Magic attack using AP, you can Manifest a Wonder at no extra AP cost so long as that Wonder’s normal AP cost matches that of the initial attack. This can also be done with RP based Martial/Magic attacks if Burst is used. The target(s) of your Wonder does not need to be the same as the Martial/Magic attack.  
- **Patron:** Whenever you rest, you can select one Wonder to become your Patron Wonder (or change your currently selected Patron Wonder). Whenever you Manifest that Wonder, you can choose to Enhance or Burst it completely for free.

# **Mental T1 Skill Trees** {#mental-t1-skill-trees}

## **Psion Arts** {#psion-arts}

**Tier 1: You gain the following passive:**

- **Sixth Sense:** You gain an extra sense known as “Psion” that starts off as Secondary. Psion as a Secondary sense allows you to detect mental energies through any surface within 100ft. This ability only informs you of the number of mental energies around you, not their location or direction. You cannot increase this sense to Primary or higher unless directly stated. Keep in mind you still need to use a Spot action to detect them in the first place.

  **Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Far Sight: Cost: *Energy equal to your total Mind*.** You can spend **2 AP** to extend the range of your psion sense to 1000ft. This also increases the range of your Ranged Manifestation option to 500ft. This effect normally only lasts until the start of your next turn, but can be maintained for another turn for no AP cost by expending the same energy cost again when your turn starts.

  **Tier 3: You gain the following passive:**

- **Primary Psion:** Psion now becomes a Primary sense for you, enabling you to use Spot to determine the direction of a chosen mental energy, as well as its total Mind.

  **Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Aura Sight: Cost: *Energy equal to half of your total Mind, rounded down*.** You can spend **2 AP** to immediately make a Spot check against all targets within your current Psion Sense range (this AP cost changes with your Spot’s AP/RP cost). This also doubles the area of your Area Manifestation option until the start of your next turn.

  **Tier 5: You gain the following passive:**

- **Heightened Psion:** Psion now becomes a Heightened sense for you, enabling your Spot check to give the individual mental stats and mental abilities (if any) of those you detect. Cannot detect abilities of a higher Stage than you’ve achieved.

## **Life (Dream)** {#life-(dream)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Bloom:** At the start of the target’s next turn, they get **50 temp HP**, lasting until the start of your next turn (can stack with itself). **Enhanced:** The temp HP applies immediately instead.  
**Verdant Soul:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you successfully hit a Manifest, you can give either the target or yourself **10 temp HP**, lasting until the start of your next turn.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Pollinate:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your PON min, rounded down.*** Whenever one of your Life Modes would activate on a target, you can use this ability to attempt to spread it to another target within 100ft of you that you can sense, requiring an attack roll. This spread cannot trigger Pollinate, and it applies the effect of that Mode immediately.

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Flourish:** At the start of the target’s next turn, their dodge rolls get **\+1 die size**, lasting until the start of your next turn. Does not stack. **Enhanced:** The bonus to their dodge dice applies immediately instead.

**Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Perennial:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your PON min.*** Whenever one of your Life Modes would expire on a target that you can sense within 100ft of you, you can use this ability. Instantly reapply that effect to them at no AP/RP cost, requiring no attack roll, and applying immediately without needing to be enhanced.

**Tier 5: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Renewal:** At the start of the target’s next turn, they restore **4 health**, only restoring health lost since the start of your last turn. This can also be used on a target who has died since the start of your last turn, the effect triggering when their turn would have started. If this effect raises them above 0 health, they are resurrected. **Enhanced:** The healing applies immediately instead.

## **Death (Nightmare)** {#death-(nightmare)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Wither:** Removes **1d12 health** from a living target. This effect is reduced by anything that blocks Supernatural damage, Temp HP, Shielding, Shrouds, and Ward effects. **Enhanced:** This damage also removes that much Max HP from the target.  
**Mortal Coil:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you successfully hit a Manifest, you can steal **1d8** **health** from the target(s), becoming Temp HP for yourself that lasts until the start of your next turn.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Fester:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your SNA min.*** Whenever you successfully Manifest a Death Mode, you can use this ability to cause that Mode’s effect (including its Enhance effect if it was applied) to reapply to the same target at the start of their next turn. This reapplication does not require an attack roll, and does not count as a Manifestation.

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Waste:** Store a Waste charge on the target, lasting until the start of your next turn. Whenever the target makes a dodge roll, you can consume up to one Waste charge on them to reduce that roll’s **die size by 2**. **Enhanced:** The affected dodge roll also has Disadvantage for this charge.

**Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Reap:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your SNA min.*** Whenever one of your Death Modes would critically hit a living target, you can use this ability to instantly attempt to Manifest a Death Mode of your choosing of the same Range type for no AP/RP cost (if the initial was Bursted, this one is Bursted for free).

**Tier 5: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Execute:** Raises the target’s **Pain Threshold by** **1d12**, lasting until the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** If the target’s health falls below half of their raised Pain Threshold while Execute is on them, they instantly lose all remaining health and die.

## **Order (Dream)** {#order-(dream)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Decree:** Store a Decree charge on the target, lasting until the start of your next turn. When the target makes a roll, you can consume up to one Decree charge on them to change that roll to instead be a static result, equal to half of their roll’s max result, **plus or minus 1** (your choice on Manifest). **Enhanced:** The baseline is set from your relevant die’s halfway point, instead of the target’s.  
**Balance:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you make an attack or dodge roll, you can **add 1** to the result, or **remove 1** from the opposing side’s result.

**Tier 2: You gain the following passive:**

- **Permanence:** One Order Mode charge you have placed on each target no longer decays at the start of your turn, lasting instead until it is consumed. Only one charge can be affected by this per target.

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Mandate:** Store a Mandate charge on the target, lasting until the start of your next turn. When the target gets a result (from a roll or from Decree), you can consume any number of Mandate charges on them to give that result **plus or minus 1** (your choice on Manifest) per charge consumed. **Enhanced:** This effect is instead activated on a damage roll, changing its damage to be **plus or minus 10** **damage** of its type if it does so.

**Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Preordained:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your PON min.*** When you finish a rest, roll one of your dodge dice, that result is your Preordained Number. Whenever you consume an Order charge, you can use this ability to additionally increase/decrease that effect by your Preordained Number (Mandate’s Enhance 10x’s this effect as damage instead).

**Tier 5: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Verdict:** Store a Verdict charge on the target, lasting until the start of your next turn. When the target lands a regular hit or a crit, you can consume up to one Verdict charge on them to swap the result to be the other, and **add/subtract 5** **damage** to the result (this doubles if the result is a crit). **Enhanced:** Also apply a stack of Strengthened or Weakened to that attack.

## **Chaos (Nightmare)** {#chaos-(nightmare)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Fracture:** Store a Fracture charge on the target, lasting until the start of your next turn. When the target makes a roll, you can consume up to one Fracture charge on them to force a reroll, with the new roll’s die size **increased or decreased by 3** (your choice on Manifest, dodge dice are affected by half, the new roll does not inherit any effects the original roll had such as Advantage/Disadvantage). **Enhanced:** The forced reroll retains any effects the original roll had, such as Advantage/Disadvantage.  
**Unbound:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you get a crit on a Manifest, you can instantly for free apply one Chaos Mode charge of your choice onto that Manifest’s target.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Ricochet:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your SNA min, rounded down.*** When you successfully Manifest a Chaos Mode, you can use this ability to place a copy of that charge onto a random valid target within range (this copies the chosen effect exactly).

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Larceny:** Store a Larceny charge on the target, lasting until the start of your next turn. When the target makes a roll, you can consume up to one Larceny charge on them to steal that roll and any effects it would have, forcing them to roll a die whose size is **increased or decreased by 1** (dodge dice are affected by half). You can use their roll, including its effects, to replace any roll you would make prior to the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** You now instead steal the action they were doing itself, lasting up to a minute. Their side functions the same (forced reroll), but now you can do their action exactly as they were attempting to by spending RP equal to its AP/RP cost, following the same rules they would as well as using their stats/dice.

**Tier 4: You gain the following passive:**

- **Control:** Once per charge, you can reroll one extra roll made by a Chaos Mode. If you do, you must use that new result.

**Tier 5: You gain the following passive:**

- **Entropy:** Fracture charges can now be used for the following effect: when the target would crit or be crit (can still occur on things with no crit effect), you can consume up to one Fracture charge on them to give them an additional roll (if critting, the additional roll is either an extra attack, or forcing them to redo the attack. If being crit, the additional roll is a new attempt to dodge. Either way, the roll does not inherit any effects from the original roll) with its die size **increased or decreased by 3** (dodge dice are affected by half). **Enhanced:** The extra reroll retains any effects the original roll had, such as Advantage/Disadvantage.

## **Beyond (Dream)** {#beyond-(dream)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Herald:** On hit, apply **6d8 Force** to the target in a direction of your choice. This Force ignores the half-max HP subtraction, instead converting directly into feet pushed after being divided by 10\. **Enhanced:** The Force also ignores Lift, treating it as if it were 0\.  
**Gust:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you successfully hit a Manifest, you can apply **5d8 Force** to whatever the target(s) of the Manifest are in any direction of your choosing. This Force adds up with any Force the Manifest itself would produce.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Momentum:** **Cost: *Energy equal to your PON min.*** Whenever a creature or object you moved with a Beyond Mode collides with something, you can use this ability. Apply **5d8 Force** to the target again in a different direction of your choosing, requiring no attack roll. This can chain with itself.

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Redirect:** Store a Redirect charge (no attack roll needed) with the range of the AP/RP you spent (Melee, Ranged, Area), lasting until the start of your next turn. When an attack hits within that range that you can sense, you can consume up to one Redirect charge of that range to make an attack roll against that attack’s result. On hit, that attack deals **20 less damage**. If reduced to 0, you can redirect it to another target within that charge’s range, requiring a new attack roll, repeating that attack's effect on hit. **Enhanced:** The intercept roll automatically hits, but only **reduces damage by 10**.

**Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Kinetic Focus:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your PON min, rounded down.*** When you make a Herald or Ascend attack roll, or the attack roll for Redirect’s extra attack, you can use this ability. Give that attack roll a stack of Advantage. This effect can be used as much as desired per attack.

**Tier 5: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Ascend:** On hit, grant the target **60 Lift**, lasting until the start of your next turn. The target is in control of their flight directly, and must stabilize for 3 RP if they take more than 1/10th of their max HP as damage, or are affected by enough force to push them. **Enhanced:** You now control their stabilization, either causing them to automatically fail or succeed on the check as desired.

## **Below (Nightmare)** {#below-(nightmare)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Sink:** Must target a grounded creature. On hit, the ground below the target grapples them, lasting until they break free with a counter grapple check of **2 or higher,** or the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** The effect now lasts for 3 turns, or until broken free from.  
**Weight:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you successfully hit a Manifest, you can apply **15 Slow stacks** to whatever the target(s) of the Manifest are, lasting until the start of your next turn.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Vice:** **Cost: *Energy equal to half of your SNA min, rounded down.*** Whenever a creature fails a counter grapple check against one of your Below Modes, you can use this ability. They take **1d12 physical** **damage**. Usable once per failed instance.

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Burden:** On hit, the target gets two Disadvantages split among either their attack or dodge rolls (your choice on Manifest, has no effect on counter grapple checks), until they break free with a counter grapple check of **3 or higher**, or the start of your next turn. This effect stacks with itself (both of the types of Disadvantage, as well as more Disadvantage on one type), but they are all removed at once with a successful counter grapple check. **Enhanced:** The effect now lasts for 3 turns, or until broken free from.

**Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Quicksand:** **Cost: *Energy equal to double your SNA min.*** Whenever a creature would successfully break free from one of your Below Mode effects while they are within 100ft of you and you can sense them, you can use this ability. Make an attack roll against that target, on hit they instead fail their counter grapple check in that instance. Usable once per breakout instance.

**Tier 5: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Entomb:** Can only be used on a target affected by both Sink and at least 4 Burden stacks. Completely entomb the target in the ground below them, clearing their Sink and Burden effects but causing them to be blind, deaf, and unable to smell, lasting until the start of your next turn. While encased, they can attempt a counter grapple check of **4 or higher** to escape their entombment. Additionally, at the start of each of their turns while entombed, they suffer **3d10 physical damage**. **Enhanced:** The effect now lasts for 3 turns, or until broken free from.

# **Mental T2 Skill Trees** {#mental-t2-skill-trees}

## **Ponderance Arts** {#ponderance-arts}

**Tier 1: You gain the following ability:**

- **Premeditate: Cost: *Energy equal to your Scaling Stat min.*** When you would apply the effects of a Wonder, you can instead use this ability. That Wonder is stored as an effect on the target requiring Concentration, its effect only activating when a preset action of your choosing occurs by or to that character, such as attacking, dodging, or moving. In addition, the effects of that Wonder are Strengthened. The stored effect ends at the start of your next turn if not used. 

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Mantra: Cost: *Energy equal to half of your Scaling Stat min, rounded down.*** When your Tenet triggers, you can use this ability. Double its bolded effect for this instance.

**Tier 3: You gain the following passive:**

- **Composure:** When you would lose your Concentration from taking damage, you instead lose only one effect you are Concentrating on, of your choice. 

**Tier 4: You gain the following passive:**

- **Forethought:** Your selected Tenet automatically applies to each of your Premeditated Wonders, if it can. This does not count against that Tenet’s once per round limit. 

**Tier 5: You gain the following passive:**

- **Daydreaming:** One of your active Concentrations cannot be broken, even if you fall unconscious. Losing Concentration still requires you to pick one to lose if you have more than one active.

## **Creation (Dream)** {#creation-(dream)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Forge:** The attack roll for this Manifest is made against yourself. On hit, create a real Martial Weapon or set of Armor of a single type, with a material rarity of either Common or Uncommon, and a **grade of 1**. The item is created at a place within your chosen range (Area creates multiple per target, each requiring a separate dodge roll from yourself against the same attack roll), and it appears either in a willing target's free hand, or at the feet of an unwilling target/a target that cannot wield it. The item lasts until the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** The created Weapon/Armor can be of Rare rarity instead.  
**Alter:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round when you hit a Manifest, choose an object the target is wearing or holding. All damage dealt to it is either Strengthened or Weakened, lasting until the start of your next turn.

**Tier 2: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Conjure:** The attack roll for this Manifest is made against yourself. On hit, create a real Magic Foci or Shroud of a single type, with Affixes of your choice up to the item’s cap of either Common or Uncommon rarity, and a **grade of 1**. The item is created at a place within your chosen range (Area creates multiple per target, each requiring a separate dodge roll from yourself against the same attack roll), and it appears either in a willing target's free hand, or at the feet of an unwilling target/a target that cannot wield it. The item lasts until the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** The created Affixes can be of Rare rarity instead.

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Consecrate:** The attack roll for this Manifest is made against yourself. On hit, create a real Mental Icon of a single Form with either Common or Uncommon rarity, and a **grade of 1**. The item is created at a place within your chosen range (Area creates multiple per target, each requiring a separate dodge roll from yourself against the same attack roll), and it appears either in a willing target's free hand, or at the feet of an unwilling target/a target that cannot wield it. In addition, if it appeared in a willing target’s hand, you can automatically attune it to them to a Tenet either you or the target have access to (must match the Pole of the Icon’s Form). The item lasts until the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** The created Icon can be of Rare rarity instead.

**Tier 4: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Fabricate:** The attack roll for this Manifest is made against yourself. On hit, create a real non-archetypal object of any shape, made of a singular material that is either Powder, Liquid, or Soft, with a **Body of up to 15**. You must know how to make the material yourself, or be familiar enough with it (up to GM) to Manifest it. The item is created at a place within your chosen range (Area creates multiple per target, each requiring a separate dodge roll from yourself against the same attack roll), and it appears either in a willing target's free hand, at the feet of an unwilling target/a target that cannot wield it, or at an unoccupied space where it can fit. The item lasts until the start of your next turn. **Enhanced:** The created object can be made of a Hard material instead.

**Tier 5: You gain the following action:**

- **Rite:** You can alternately Manifest one of your Creation Modes as a Rite. Doing so requires 2 hours if the item is Archetypal and Common, or Non-Archetypal and Powder/Liquid (Rare/Hard items cannot be made permanent through Rite). It instead takes 8 hours if it's Archetypal and Uncommon, or Non-Archetypal and Soft. During the Rite, you are considered doing a heavy activity and have zero energy (you can cancel the Rite at any time, losing all progress). On completion, the item is made permanent, though it can still be dismissed by yourself, or by others who identify it as Mentally made.

## **Destruction (Nightmare)** {#destruction-(nightmare)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode and Tenet:**  
**Corrode:** On hit, deals **2d6 acid damage**, applying Stain stacks equal to the damage dealt. The damage and stains applied by this Mode are Strengthened against objects. **Enhanced:** The Stains applied are instead Solid Stains, taking 6 AP to remove and are removed separately.  
**Infuse:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. ## **Ruin (Nightmare)** {#ruin-(nightmare)}

**Tier 1: You gain the following Mode, passive, and Tenet:**

- **Jinx:** On hit, store a Jinx of **1d10 physical damage** on the target. **Enhanced:** The first time this Jinx would fizzle, it waits for the target’s next attack roll instead.  
- **Ruin’s Bands:** Ruin Modes cannot crit. Instead, on hit, they store their effect on the target as a Jinx, lasting until the start of your next turn. The target’s next attack roll resolves every Jinx on it at once. The result depends on where that roll lands relative to its maximum:   
  - **Under one half:** All the Jinxes fizzle.  
  - **One half to under three quarters:** The Jinxes’ effects gain 2 stacks of Strengthened  
  - **Three quarters or higher:** The Jinxes’ effects gain 4 stacks of Strengthened  
- **Doom:** Only active while this is your selected Tenet. Once per round at any time as a free reaction you can roll the Ruin Table found at the end of this tree, then select a target you can sense within 100ft of you to apply the rolled effect to on a successful attack roll.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Gild: Cost: *Energy equal to half your SNA min, rounded down.*** When you Manifest a Ruin Mode, you can use this ability. If that Manifest hits, the roll that resolves the target’s Jinxes has Advantage. 

**Tier 3: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Forfeit:** On hit, store a Jinx that **removes** **4 energy** from the target. Any energy that would go below 0 becomes health loss at 1:1 instead (reduced by anything that blocks Supernatural damage, Temp HP, Shielding, Shrouds, and Ward effects). **Enhanced:** That conversion becomes 1:2. 

**Tier 4: You gain the following ability:**

- **Reversal: Cost: *Energy equal to half your SNA min, rounded down.*** After a Jinxed creature’s roll resolves your Jinxes, you can use this ability to flip that roll: that die’s result becomes (its size \+ 1 − the result). 

**Tier 5: You gain the following Mode:**

- **Calamity:** On hit, roll a d100 and store the Ruin Table result on the target as a Jinx. **Enhanced:** Roll twice and choose which result to store.

# **Mental T5 Skill Trees** {#mental-t5-skill-trees}

## **Esoteric Arts** {#esoteric-arts}

**Tier 1: You gain the following action and passive:**

- **Seed:** Spend 8 hours in a Trance to seed an Omen of a Wonder you have at Tier 5\. It is bound to you and counts as one of your Concentrations. At Seeding, choose its Form from its Wonder’s pole (Common, Uncommon, or Rare) and its Alignment depth.  
- **Omen:** Your Omen has its own sapience, initiative, and turn, with its own AP, RP, and an energy pool equal to 5 times its skill points. It has stat points equal to yours, with two thirds in its Wonder’s scaling stat and one third in Willpower (you can flip this split at Seeding), and skill points equal to one fifth of its stat points, which only set its dice. It always has full Mental Theory and its own Wonder at Tier 5, regardless of its skill points.  
- **Pole-Bound:** Your Omen Manifests only its own Wonder, and that Wonder’s Tenet is always active for it. It is its own Icon, using its Form’s Ward without needing one. Attacks between it and creatures or effects of its opposite pole are Strengthened, in both directions. It never acts against its pole and never aids the opposite one.  
- **Bond:** If you end this Concentration yourself and your Omen is willing, it is dismissed. It is willing if, since it was seeded, you have Manifested its Wonder more times than its opposite pole’s Wonder. Otherwise, make an attack roll against it; on a miss, the bond is severed and it turns hostile. If you lose this Concentration any other way, it fights on your side for the rest of that battle, but turns hostile if angered (you or an ally harming it, Manifesting its opposite pole where it can sense it, or acting against its pole). If it is not hostile when the battle ends, you can rebind it with 1 hour of work.

**Tier 2: You gain the following ability:**

- **Channel:** **Cost: Energy equal to half your scaling stat min, rounded down.** When you Manifest a Wonder, you can use this ability to Manifest it from your Omen’s position instead of your own, using its senses. If the Mode belongs to your Omen’s Wonder, its attack roll has Advantage.

**Tier 3: You gain the following ability:**

- **Unbind:** **Cost: 2 AP and energy equal to your scaling stat min.** Until the start of your next turn, your Omen has its full stats (stat points equal to twice yours, with its skill points unchanged). Its current HP rises with its Max HP, and when this ends, its current HP is reduced to its normal Max HP if it is above it.

**Tier 4: You gain the following passive:**

- **Kinship:** While your Omen is within 30ft of you, you also benefit from its Tenet. If it is your selected Tenet, you can trigger it one additional time per round (your Omen still triggers its own once per round).

**Tier 5: You gain the following action:**

- **Rend:** Summon an Omen of a Wonder you have at Tier 5 instantly. Make an attack roll against it with Disadvantage, with it dodging at Advantage. On a hit, it is seeded and bound to you as normal. On a miss, it arrives free with its full stats and is hostile.
