Magic balance review

Five passes, one per Magic Theory tier: pure casters against caster monsters built from the same schools. What won, what did the damage, and what looks off.

30 fights 5 passes (T1–T5) Pain Threshold ½ 24 Oct 2026
How the sim ran

The same five-character party as the Martial passes (90 stat points and 30 Skill Points each), now as pure casters: Magic trees only, an attuned Foci and Shroud, armor, no weapons. Bulwark is the tank, Warden the support, the others casters on Grasp, Reach or both (the Raw caster has no Foci). Each pass took the schools one Magic Theory tier unlocks up to T5. The Swarm (ten goblins), Raiders (five) and Ogre Magus boss cast from the same schools, scaled to their Skill Points.

The AI casts through the real Cast Spell path: one support spell a turn (Shield, Burden, Repulsion), then its best spell per AP on the weakest foe in range, adding Mods while the Energy lasts. Tier 3 adds Poison coats, Charm and Hex; Tier 4 keeps one creation up each turn; Tier 5 adds Restore, Mirage and Shift.

Summary

Nine findings

Look at

Animations grow with the square of their points

An Animation's health is the material's HP per point × its points × its Size, and its Size comes from the Body of the pile, which must be at least the points. So more points also means a bigger Size. The T4 Animator's Stone Animate (Expanded Animation) came out at 2,643 HP per cast, renewed every turn: about the whole party's HP (2,835). Make + Animate with Clay made 882 HP ones. They hit for 80–90 a swing, against 13 for a Form Summon (212–342 HP).

Monsters attacked them first, so in 3 of 6 T4 fights the party took no damage at all. The party won all three fights where its Animators used rotation 2 (Make + Animate, Form + Animate).

Look at

Tier 2 fights are won by stacks, not hits

In T2 the party lost 8,078 HP and only about 1,940 of it was direct spell damage: the rest was Ignite, Stain and Electric Stain burning at the end of turns. Flame + Frost and Crackle + Glob deal no direct damage at all and still carried the goblin swarm, ten casters stacking on the same targets. The party won only the boss fights (2 of 6), and fights averaged 8 rounds.

Look at

Tier 3 schools can't finish a fight

Grasp Arcana, Venomancy, Charm and Witchery have no direct-damage Core, so each caster carried a T1 school at T2 (a judgment call; see below). The party won 0 of 6 and averaged 9.5 rounds. Against the Ogre Magus they dealt 83 and 443 damage in 14 and 13 rounds: its Heavy Iron and Carapace soaked the carrier spells, and its Hexes hurt every party member who attacked. The afflictions themselves are strong: Poison, Charm and Hex checks took 560–1,204 HP off the monsters per non-boss fight, more than direct hits did.

Look at

Restore heals 8 a cast, and its time window reads oddly

Restore heals 2 × Power: 8 per cast at Power 4. The Mender cast it 56 times for 448 HP in all, while a single Strike averaged 56 damage. Two code questions sit under it. The system reads "since the start of your last turn" as the start of the caster's current turn, so a Restore on your own turn heals only what was lost during it (the sim used the start of the previous turn instead). And heals aren't taken off the loss, so two Restores can heal more than was lost in the window.

Watch

Combos lead Tier 1

Force + Cut averaged 42.4 per cast, Force + Slam 31.5 and Force + Stab 25.3, against 20.7 for Cut alone. Once a caster knows two schools, the AI never cast a single Core except as a fallback. T1 was the most even pass: 4 of 6 wins in 4.7 rounds.

Watch

Strike is the strongest damage spell in the passes

Arcanomancy's Strike averaged 56 per cast for both sides, and the party hit 88% of the time. Shift with Toss came next at 37–39. Monster Strikers made the T5 swarm and raider fights short losses (4–8 rounds). What Mirage + Strike does to monster casters (Disadvantage and Weakened on Magic) isn't measured separately.

Watch

Squishy casters die before they act

Monsters go for the lowest HP in range, and the Raw caster (Con 30, Light armor, no Foci) often went down in round 1 before its first turn: 0 turns in the first fight of T3, T4 and T5. The Grasp and Reach casters in Light armor followed. A pure caster party has no one to hold the line except Shields and creations.

Watch

Afflictions and burns go uncredited at the table too

Poison ticks, Charm and Hex self-damage and stack burns land on their own cards, separate from the spell that caused them. In the sim they only show as HP lost outside direct hits. At the table that makes it hard to see what a Venomancer or a Heat caster contributed.

Fine

Bosses are beatable, as intended

The party won all 8 boss fights outside T3, in 2–6 rounds. A lone Ogre Magus casting the same schools can't keep up with five casters. The one exception is T3, where the party had no way through its armor.

Read with care

Sim judgment calls and gaps

Choices the sim made

  • T3 carrier school: every T3 caster spent its last 3 SP on Cut, Stab or Slam at T2, so Poison had something to ride on.
  • Restore's window: the start of the caster's previous turn, not the current one.
  • Creations: one two-handed Heavy natural weapon for Arm, Skin at Medium, Strength-heavy points, the best Heavy weapon Make allows.
  • Shift: material always at hand; Harden (Strengthened) and Muddy (their attacks at Disadvantage) added on when affordable.
  • Mirage: Mirage + Strike when known, otherwise Fidelity for Disadvantage on attack rolls. Aura was left out (it would catch allies).

Gaps in the numbers

  • Burns and afflictions aren't credited to a spell; they show as HP lost outside direct hits.
  • T1 and T2 can't split spells by side (both sides used the same ones), so their table is pooled.
  • The AI spends AP on afflictions even when they do little (Charm on an Ogre that never attacks), so T3's damage is a floor.
  • A harmless server error (an update to a token that was already gone) showed up in a few fights; none changed the outcome.
For the designer

Questions

  1. Restore's window. Answered and fixed in v0.79.5: on your own turn the window starts at your previous turn.
  2. Restore stacking. Answered and fixed in v0.79.5: what Restore already healed counts against the window.
  3. Restore's size. 2 × Power (8 at Power 4) is a fraction of what one damage spell deals. Intended for a T5 school?
  4. Animation health. Is health meant to grow with points × Size, when Size itself grows with the points? A Stone Animation at T4 outlasts a whole party.
  5. Tier 2 stacks. Are burns meant to out-damage direct hits this much, especially from a swarm stacking one target?
  6. Tier 3 damage. Should a T3 school be able to deal damage on its own, or is pairing it with a damage school the intended build?
